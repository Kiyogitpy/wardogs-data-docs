About Wardogs

Wardogs is a team shooter with infantry, vehicles and base building. Three factions, Lonestar, Valkyra and Manticore, fight over zones and objectives in modes such as King of the Hill and Control Zone, with a sniper mode, deathmatch, PvE missions and a shooting range alongside.

Players carry rifles, pistols, shotguns, launchers and bows, drive and fly helicopters, tanks and trucks, and put up bases and emplacements with hammers and Build Supplies.
