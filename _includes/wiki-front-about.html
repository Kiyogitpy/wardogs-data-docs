About Wardogs

Wardogs is a team shooter with infantry, vehicles and base building. Its three named factions are Lonestar, Valkyra and Manticore. Season 3 adds Limited Mode and the Sabatulo city map. Other articles cover King of the Hill, Control Zone, sniper modes, deathmatch, PvE missions and the shooting range; individual pages distinguish saved settings from established availability.

Players carry rifles, pistols, shotguns, launchers and bows, drive and fly helicopters, tanks and trucks, and put up bases and emplacements with hammers and Build Supplies.
