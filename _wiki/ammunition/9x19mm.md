The 9x19mm caliber is used by 6 weapons, across the Submachine gun and Pistol classes. Its rounds are Standard (FMJ), Flesh Damage (HP), Armor Piercing (AP). The tables below show how each type does against armor.

## Weapons that use it

| Weapon | Class | Fire rate | Upper torso, no armor, point blank |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| [AMP-9](../weapons/amp-9.md) | Submachine gun | 900 rounds/min | 25.4 |
| [MP5](../weapons/mp5.md) | Submachine gun | 800 rounds/min | 25.4 |

Point-blank figures use standard rounds (buckshot for shotguns, per pellet), per hit, without falloff. See [Ammunition](../ammunition.md) for the bullet types.

## Ammunition types

Upper torso hits from the AMP-9 at point blank range, per hit, without falloff. Armor changes these numbers a lot, so every armor class is shown.

| Ammo | No armor | Light | Medium | Heavy | Super heavy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Standard (FMJ) | 25.4 | 17.8 | 15.2 | 12.7 | 10.2 |
| Flesh Damage (HP) | 50.8 | 7.5 | 5.5 | 3.4 | 1.4 |
| Armor Piercing (AP) | 20.3 | 16.1 | 14.6 | 13.2 | 11.8 |
| Standard (FMJ) with tracer | 25.4 | 17.8 | 15.2 | 12.7 | 10.2 |

Flesh Damage (HP) with tracer, Armor Piercing (AP) with tracer match their base type.

## Against armor

- Armor Piercing does less damage than standard rounds up to medium armor and more damage from heavy armor up.
- Flesh Damage does 2.0 times the standard damage with no armor and 0.1 times against super heavy armor. It is the strongest type against unarmored targets. It is the weakest type against super heavy armor.

The numbers come from the upper torso column of the table above. Head hits follow the same pattern at higher values; see [Weapon Damage](../../game-features/Wardogs_WeaponDamage_v1.md).
