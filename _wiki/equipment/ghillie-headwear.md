Ghillie Headwear is camouflage for the head. It must be worn with the [Ghillie Body Suit](ghillie-body-suit.md). Like the suit, it removes the death cam after the wearer kills an enemy, and it hides the wearer from infrared range finders. The item data gives it no damage reduction, so how much damage it stops is not yet known.

## Overview

The shop groups the Ghillie Headwear under "Camouflage" and lists it as armor. It goes in the Helmet slot.

The Item Shop also lists a "Ghillie Headwear (Dead)" at 2,800 cash. The data does not say how it differs from this item. The item data gives no weight, so the weight is not yet known. For all the vests and helmets, see [Body Armor](body-armor.md).

## Protection

The item data gives the Ghillie Headwear no damage reduction and no covered body parts. The damage tables on the Weapon Damage page treat it as no armor, because its real effect on damage is not determined in the data. How much protection it gives is not yet known.
