The Halligan Bar is a quick-slot tool. Its main job is to pry open doors. It has limited durability, and it does nothing to gates.

## Overview

The shop text says the Halligan Bar pries open doors but is ineffective against gates, and that it has limited durability. Its durability was 5,000 in Season 1 and is 7,000 in Season 2. The sources do not say what happens when it runs out, so that is not yet known.

## How it works

- Using it on a door forces the door open. The item data names this as a separate action.
- The item data also gives the bar a vehicle unlock action, which forces open a locked vehicle. The sources give no more detail on it.
- It can also be swung as a melee weapon. The item data gives the hit 100 damage.
- The bar cannot be used while the player is critically injured or dead.
- Inspecting the item interrupts a use.
