Level 2 Helmet is the second of the four helmet tiers. It protects the head and cuts head damage by 40%. It costs 500 cash and needs no unlock.

## Overview

Level 2 Helmet sits between [Level 1 Helmet](level-1-helmet.md) and [Level 3 Helmet](level-3-helmet.md). The Item Shop describes it as "Medium Damage Reduction" and says it protects the head. It does not cover the neck.

It goes in the Helmet slot and weighs 1.6. For all the vests and helmets, see [Body Armor](body-armor.md).

## Protection

Level 2 Helmet reduces damage to the head by 40%. It does nothing for the neck or the body. The item data gives it 100 durability.

With M4 full-metal-jacket rounds, a head hit does 65.8 HP with no helmet and 39.5 HP with Level 2 Helmet.

| Helmet | Damage reduction | Body parts covered | Durability |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| [Level 1 Helmet](level-1-helmet.md) | 30% | Head | 80 |
| **Level 2 Helmet** | 40% | Head | 100 |
| [Level 3 Helmet](level-3-helmet.md) | 50% | Head and neck | 180 |
| [Level 4 Helmet](level-4-helmet.md) | 60% | Head and neck | 200 |
| [Pilot Helmet](pilot-helmet.md) | 30% | Head | 180 |
| [Tank Helmet](tank-helmet.md) | 55% | Head and neck | 180 |

The data gives four durability stages, and all four list the same reduction. How durability is used up is not yet known.

## Season 2 changes

The Season 2 data does not change Level 2 Helmet.
