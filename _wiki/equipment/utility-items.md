# Utility Items

| Utility items | |
|---|---|
| Groups | Parachutes, backpacks, tactical vests, recon optics, tools, batteries, fuel and supplies |
| Price range | 0 (Pouch) to 4,000 cash (Arsenal Backpack and 2 Slings) |
| Unlock level | 0 or 1 for everything listed |
{: .wiki-infobox }

Utility items are the gear that is neither armor, medical, explosive nor buildable. This group covers the parachutes used for dropping in, the backpacks and tactical vests that set how much a player can carry, the optics used for spotting, and the tools used to repair vehicles, open doors and build structures.

## Overview

Each item below has its own article with the full details. The tables on this page give the shop prices and the main numbers in one place. Where a number is missing from the data, the article says so.

## Effects

### Parachutes

A parachute is a single-use item that must be deployed by hand. The data gives it a minimum fall before it can be opened, a minimum clearance above the ground, and an open time. See [Parachutes](parachutes.md) for the values and their units.

| Item | Weight (kg) | Price (cash) | Can steer | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Basic Parachute | 2.5 | 500 | no | No glide; no pull-up |
| Sport Parachute | 9 | 700 | yes | Glide, pull-up and speed caps; pull-up uses stamina |

### Backpacks

A backpack adds a grid of storage slots. The Pouch is free, and the other standard backpacks cost from 350 to 1,600 cash. The bags with a sling slot are on [Sling Backpacks](sling-backpacks.md).

| Backpack | Price (cash) | Grid | Slots |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Pouch | 0 | 3 x 2 (vertical placement only) | 6 |
| Scout Backpack | 350 | 4 x 2 | 8 |
| Field Backpack | 650 | 6 x 2 | 12 |
| Operator Backpack | 800 | 5 x 3 | 15 |
| Assault Backpack | 1,200 | 5 x 5 | 21 |
| Ruck Backpack | 1,600 | 6 x 4 | 24 |

| Sling backpack | Price (cash) | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Medium Backpack and Sling | 1,400 (hidden in the shop) | One sling for a weapon or special item |
| Gunner Backpack and Sling | 2,400 | 24 slots; one sling slot for equipment |
| Arsenal Backpack and 2 Slings | 4,000 | 24 slots; two sling slots for equipment |

### Tactical vests

Tactical vests are not body armor. They give no damage reduction in the data. Each one unlocks more quick slots and speeds up reloading and switching to utility items. None adds storage. For armor, see [Body Armor](body-armor.md).

| Vest | Price (cash) | Quick slots unlocked | Reload and utility speed |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Small Tac Vest | 100 | 5 and 6 | minor increase |
| Medium Tac Vest | 250 | 5 to 8 | moderate increase |
| Large Tac Vest | 400 | all | strong increase |

### Recon optics

These optics spot enemy infantry and vehicles at a distance. The shop text describes what they show. The data gives no spotting range for any of them.

| Item | Price (cash) | Magnification |
|---|---:|---|
| Binoculars | 180 | low |
| Monocular | 350 | medium, variable |
| Range Finder | 1,450 | high, variable; shows an accurate distance |
| Infrared Range Finder | 3,200 | infrared, variable; shows an accurate distance |

### Tools

| Item | Price (cash) | What it does |
|---|---:|---|
| Halligan Bar | 885 | Pries open doors, but not gates. Durability 7,000 in Season 2 |
| Wrench | 180 | Repairs all vehicles, slowly |
| Light Drill | 600 | Repairs vehicles at a moderate speed. Needs batteries |
| Heavy Drill | 1,300 | Repairs vehicles at the fastest speed. Needs batteries |

The Light and Heavy Drills use Batteries, and Batteries also power the [Defibrillator](defibrillator.md). Two sizes exist:

| Item | Price (cash) | Capacity |
|---|---:|---|
| Battery | 150 | 20 V, 5 AH |
| High Capacity Battery | 300 | 20 V, 15 AH |

### Vehicle fuel

The 40 L Fuel Canister costs 150 cash. It has 40 durability, and each use adds 25 fuel, according to the item data. See [Fuel Canister](fuel-canister.md).

### Build tools

Hammers are the build tools. The Small Hammer costs 100 cash, the Medium Hammer 800 and the Large Hammer 2,400. Each one needs Build Supplies to build, and the Large Hammer can place every structure. See [Hammers](hammers.md).

### Supplies

Supplies are used up by structures and the Forward Operating Base, and the FOB supplies need a Forward Operating Base radius to use. Each one is sold in lots of 50 for 10 cash. See [Supplies](supplies.md) for what each one does.
