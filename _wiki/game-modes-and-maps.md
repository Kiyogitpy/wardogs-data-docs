# Game Modes & Maps

Wardogs has a few team modes built around zones and objectives, a sniper mode with a stillness rule, a deathmatch mode, a set of PvE missions, a shooting range for practice, and two Season 2 additions, Team Offence and the Hot Potato pre-lobby mini-game (see also [Pre-lobby](game-modes-and-maps/pre-lobby.md)). This hub covers each mode and the maps they are played on.

Where the data has no answer, the articles say "not yet known" rather than guess. That includes team sizes (a server setting for most modes) and match timers for the PvE missions, which have no time limit in their data.

## Other modes in the data

The server browser lists one more mode, Tank Town. It uses the King of the Hill experience unchanged, with the same three teams and score limit, and it has no map or zone data of its own.

## Server options

Hosts can set options that apply across modes. The data names these server options: Hardcore, Infantry Only, No Rebuy, No Return To Spawn, No Vault, No Player Looting, No Team Change, No Give Up, Reduced HUD, Persistent Cash and XP (on or off), and minimum or maximum cash and player level limits. For most of them the data gives only the names. The two variants whose rules are stored are described in [Hardcore and Infantry Only](game-modes-and-maps/hardcore-and-infantry-only.md).
