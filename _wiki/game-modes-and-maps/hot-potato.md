Hot Potato is a mode added in Season 2. The data shows that it has three Hot Potato items, its own map tracker and its own animation set. Beyond that, the sources do not describe how the mode is played.

## Overview

Hot Potato came in with Season 2 alongside [Team Offence](team-offence.md). The sources give it an item name, a short description key and a map tracker, but no finished display names, so the item names are shown as the data labels them.

## Rules and objectives

- **Items.** Three items exist: Alpha, Bravo and Charlie. The item display text is not in the data (the entry has a name key with no source text).
- **Throwables.** A Season 2 challenge row refers to a Hot Potato throwable target. This suggests that the item is thrown, but the rules are not in the sources.
- **Map tracker.** The mode has its own map tracker data.
- **Objective, scoring, teams, match length and win condition.** Not yet known.

## Rewards

The sources do not list Hot Potato-specific rewards. The general cash table applies; see [Cash and Rewards](../progression-and-economy/cash-and-rewards.md).
