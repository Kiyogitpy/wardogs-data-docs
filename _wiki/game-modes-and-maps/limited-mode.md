# Limited Mode

Season 3 introduces Limited Mode, restricting vendor purchases to an early-to-mid-season selection. The [official Season 3 patch notes](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1867240/view/688648135317127221) list these exclusions:

- Hollow Point (HP) and Armor Piercing (AP) ammunition.
- Level 3 and Level 4 armor.
- Attack vehicles.
- CIWS, Stingray, MG Nest and Drill Rig.
- Anti-tank mines.

Cash and progression earned here carry over to other modes. Limited servers have a server-browser filter. These are the developer's stated restrictions; the examples are not a reconstructed exhaustive vendor allowlist. The notes also report a fix for mode-specific vendor restrictions persisting after map rotation.
