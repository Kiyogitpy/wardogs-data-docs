# Sabatulo

The [official Season 3 patch notes](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1867240/view/688648135317127221) introduce **Sabatulo** as the first city map. It combines close street fighting with interiors and elevated positions; announced landmarks include a hospital, swimming pool, power station and river embankment.

The existing [Saburtulo data reference](saburtulo.md) uses a different spelling and discusses saved map/zone assets. Similar names alone do not establish an exact package-to-release-map identity. This page records the officially announced map and landmarks without transferring inferred map dimensions or spawn inventories to it.
