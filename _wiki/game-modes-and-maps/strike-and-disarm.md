Strike and Disarm is a PvE mission played against AI squads instead of other players. It includes a laptop hack and opens with a four-line voice-over intro. A player who dies stays out for the rest of the mission.

Strike and Disarm is a plain-language name taken from the data's internal mission name. The data gives no display name, so this is not confirmed as the in-game title.

## Overview

Strike and Disarm is one of the three PvE missions in the data, alongside [Hunter Killer](hunter-killer.md) and [VIP Escort](vip-escort.md). It is the mission with the laptop hack. The shared PvE rules are described on the [PvE Scenarios](pve-scenarios.md) page.

## Objective and rules

- **Respawns.** None. A player who dies stays out for the rest of the mission.
- **Player faction.** Set to the Manticore faction, the same faction the Manticore AI uses.
- **Laptop hack.** The mission has a laptop hack with a start line and an end line of voice-over. The hack is an interaction with its own duration in the data, but the length is not yet known.
- **Intro and completion.** Four intro voice-over lines and a mission-complete line.
- **Scenario value.** The data sets an unnamed value of 3000. Its meaning, such as a time limit or a score target, is not confirmed.
- **Map, layout and other objectives.** Not yet known.

## Enemies

The data does not link an AI squad to this mission, so the number of enemies and their squad types are not yet known. The five AI types used in the PvE missions are described on the [AI enemies](ai-enemies.md) page.

## Rewards

The mission has no reward table of its own, so its rewards are not yet known. Rewards follow the general rules; see [Cash and Rewards](../progression-and-economy/cash-and-rewards.md). The session-event reward lookup has no rows for PvE missions.
