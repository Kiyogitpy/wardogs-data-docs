# Wardogs Wiki

Welcome to the Wardogs Wiki: plain-language articles on every weapon, piece of gear, vehicle, mode and game mechanic, written from the numbers on the [Wardogs Data Reference](../index.md). Each article ends with links to the data pages it was written from, so you can always check a number at its source.

All figures are for the current live build (CL-516586, Season 2) unless an article says otherwise.
