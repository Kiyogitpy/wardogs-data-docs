# Progression & Economy

## Season 3 progression

The [official Season 3 patch notes](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1867240/view/688648135317127221) announce seasons of roughly **3 months**, increased XP requirements and slower launch progression. Armor, backpacks, tactical vests and parachutes can be unlocked through every role track. Assault gets armor earlier, Medic tactical vests, Support backpacks and Recon AP ammunition. Ammunition unlocks are spread across the relevant weapon roles and moved later on the Wardog track.

Low-level players receive a role-specific XP catch-up bonus and vendor discount that grow week by week through the season. The announcement gives no fixed percentage or weekly schedule. An Intel-based replacement was planned for the following year; that is a plan, not an available spending system.

| Announced unlock change | Earlier level | S3 level |
|---|---:|---:|
| Assault: C4 | 4 | 5 |
| Assault: Molotov | 13 | 8 |
| Support: Large Hammer | 8 | 14 |
| Support: Verba | 14 | 8 |
| Recon: AMR 50 | 35 | 41 |
| Driver: tanks | 20–35 | 35–50 |
| Wardog: tanks (announcement; L2A6 in saved tree) | 145 | 200 |

The [current Wardog table](progression-and-economy/currencies.md#current-s3-wardog-unlocks) retains individual tracked-vehicle gates: SPH-2 145, T-72B3 155, Vanguard AA 165 and L2A6 200. The announcement does not resolve that broader wording against each saved node.

The six role articles display current saved unlock costs and levels. These data rows can coexist with removed offerings: the **Wager Vendor was removed in S3**, and the Infrared Range Finder was removed from vendors pending redesign. [Wagers](progression-and-economy/wagers.md) documents retained configuration, not an earning route. [Limited Mode](game-modes-and-maps/limited-mode.md) has additional purchase restrictions, with cash and progression carrying between modes.

Players earn cash and XP for almost everything they do in a match: kills, revives, heals, vehicle work and zone play. Cash buys gear, weapons and vehicles, and XP raises each role's mastery level. This hub covers how the money and progress systems fit together.

Where the game has no answer, the articles say "not yet known". The sources do not give the level or cost that unlocks each skill rank, for example.

## Currencies

The S3 currency table defines Cash with a starting balance of 10,000, Gold Bars with 0 and Movember with 0. Passive missions separately reference Intel; its missing currency-definition entry does not establish an initial balance or a spending rule. See [Currencies](progression-and-economy/currencies.md) for the details.
