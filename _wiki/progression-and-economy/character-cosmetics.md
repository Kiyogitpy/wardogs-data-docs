# Character Cosmetics

| Character Cosmetics | |
|---|---|
| Outfits and character skins | None in the data |
| Character-carried cosmetics | 1 parachute skin (Sport parachute, Digital Wood set) |
| Currency | Gold Bars, sold on the Gold Market |
| Internal name | Sport parachute skin row in the Season 1 skin table (`DT_Cosmetics_Season01_Skins`) |
{: .wiki-infobox }

Character cosmetics are the looks a player's own character can wear or carry. The data has no outfits and no character skins. The only cosmetic in the data that a character carries is a parachute skin.

## Overview

The data's cosmetic tables cover weapons, vehicles, emotes, faction logos, bobbleheads and one taxi skin. None of them is a character outfit. The Sport parachute is the one item a character carries, so its skin is the one character-side cosmetic listed here.

## Parachute skin

| Item | Skin set | Season | Price (Gold Bars) | How it unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sport parachute | Digital Wood | Season 1 | 15 | Gold Market |

The gear shop lists a Sport parachute at 700 (see [Shops](shops.md)). The shop data does not name the currency. The skin changes how the parachute looks. The data does not say whether a player must own the parachute before buying the skin.

## Cost and unlocking

- **Gold Market.** The parachute skin is bought with Gold Bars. Gold Bars are covered in [Currencies](currencies.md).
- **No outfits.** The data gives no price, unlock or set for any outfit, because it has none.

## Not yet in the data

- Character outfits, body skins or character models with a cosmetic set.
- How a character cosmetic is equipped in a match.
