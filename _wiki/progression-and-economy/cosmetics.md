# Cosmetics

| Cosmetics | |
|---|---|
| Kinds | Weapon and vehicle skins, emotes, faction logos, bobbleheads, taxi skins |
| Season 1 | Three skin sets (Black and Gold, Digital Wood, Two-Tone Desert) and three emotes |
| Season 2 | Added a Chocolate Chip set and more skins, eight Season 2 skin rows in all |
| Currency | Gold Bars, sold on the Gold Market |
| Unlock schedule | Not yet known (see below) |
| Internal name | Cosmetic data tables and skin assets |
{: .wiki-infobox }

Cosmetics change how weapons, vehicles and emotes look. Skins repaint a weapon, vehicle or parachute with a pattern and colour set. Emotes are actions a player can perform. Most skins and emotes are priced in Gold Bars on the Gold Market.

## Overview

Cosmetics are grouped into sets. A skin set has one look, such as Digital Wood, and applies to several items. Each cosmetic row points at one item, vehicle or emote and at the set it belongs to. The data shows the look (pattern and colour) of each set, but not how a skin looks on a model.

## How it works

- **Sets.** A skin set applies to several items. The Two-Tone Desert set covers 12 Season 1 rows, Digital Wood covers 4, and Black and Gold covers 1. Season 2 adds rows to these sets and a new Chocolate Chip set.
- **Items.** The same weapon can appear in more than one set. The M4 has a Digital Wood skin and a Two-Tone Desert skin.
- **Emotes.** Each emote has a name and an action. Six emote entries exist. Three of them, Shh, Rock Paper Scissors and Middle Finger, have cosmetic rows with prices.
- **Gold Market.** The market sells skins and emotes priced in Gold Bars. Some rows have no market price in the data.
- **Unlocking.** The data does not say whether cosmetics unlock with level, with purchase only, or some other way. The season schedule that would gate them is empty in this build.

### Other cosmetic types

- **Faction logos.** Three faction logo cosmetics exist. The data gives them no price.
- **Bobbleheads.** Four bobblehead cosmetics exist. The data gives them no price.
- **Taxi skins.** A taxi cosmetic for the MH-6 exists. The data gives it no price.

## Tips that follow from the numbers

- Emotes and several Season 1 skins cost 5 Gold Bars, the lowest listed price. The single-hand emotes have no price in the data.
- The most expensive listed skin is the Season 2 Black and Gold MH-6 at 30 Gold Bars.
- Most Season 2 skins cost 10 Gold Bars.

## Season 2 changes

- Season 2 adds the Chocolate Chip set, with three skins (M14, Evolys and TCX SPR) at 10 Gold Bars each.
- The Black and Gold set gains rows for the Havoc and the Bushmaster M17S.
- The Digital Wood set gains rows for the Havoc, the MH-6 and one more weapon, and the wear threshold on ten existing rows went from 0.8 to 1.0.
- The Two-Tone Desert set gains rows for the Havoc, the KH-2002, the Bushmaster M17S and two more weapons.
