Team Offence challenges belong to the [Team Offence](../game-modes-and-maps/team-offence.md) mode. They cover punishing a kill and voting on a teamkill. The data gives no description and no amount for either.

## Challenges

| Challenge | What earns it | Cash | Role XP |
|---|---|---|---|
| Team Offence Punish | Punishing a kill in Team Offence. No description in the data. | Not in the data | Not in the data |
| Teamkill Voter | Voting on a teamkill. No description in the data. | Not in the data | Not in the data |

## Season 2 changes

- The Season 02 update added the punish challenge and the teamkill voter challenge.
