# Season 3 Twitch Drops

The [official Season 3 patch notes](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1867240/view/688648135317127221) announce nine Season 3 Twitch Drop packages. [Link your accounts](https://account.wardogs.com), watch WARDOGS for the listed time, and claim the rewards on Twitch. Claimed rewards appear in the frontend vault and can be redeemed at in-match vendors. These are the announced watch thresholds; no campaign closing date or reset schedule is specified.

| Package | Watch time | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| Walk It Off | 15 minutes | Bandage ×2 |
| Happy Hour | 1 hour | Improvised Explosive Device ×1; Molotov ×2 |
| Get In There | 2 hours | White Smoke Grenade ×2; Adrenaline Pen ×1; Field Backpack ×1 |
| Leap of Faith | 4 hours | Basic Parachute ×1; Sport Parachute ×1 |
| Knock Knock | 5 hours 30 minutes | Halligan Bar ×1; RPG7 ×1; 93mm ×2 |
| Taxi Service | 7 hours 30 minutes | Littlebird ×1; Wolf-110 ×1 |
| Fresh Recruit | 9 hours 30 minutes | Small Backpack ×1; L1 Helmet ×1; L1 Armor ×1 |
| Tactical Kit | 11 hours 30 minutes | TCX-SPR ×1; TCX-SPR 28 RND Magazine ×2; OKP 7 Reflex ×1; Rubberized Ergonomic Foregrip ×1 |
| Dog of War | 15 hours | Medium Backpack ×1; L2 Helmet ×1; L2 Armor ×1 |

The source names the Taxi Service aircraft only as Littlebird; no armed variant is inferred. Item names above preserve the announcement, including 93mm ammunition.
