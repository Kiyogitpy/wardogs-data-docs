# Vehicle Skins

| Vehicle Skins | |
|---|---|
| Skin sets | Two-Tone Desert, Black and Gold, Taxi |
| Vehicle skins | 6 priced rows, plus 1 taxi skin and 4 bobbleheads with no price |
| Currency | Gold Bars, sold on the Gold Market |
| Internal name | Vehicle skin tables and the taxi skin data (`DT_Cosmetics_Season01_Skins`, `DT_Cosmetics_Season02_Skins`, `DT_Cosmetics_Taxi`, `DT_Bobblehead_Faction_Cosmetics`) |
{: .wiki-infobox }

A vehicle skin repaints a vehicle with a pattern and colour set. Most vehicle skins are bought on the Gold Market for 5 to 30 Gold Bars. The taxi skin and the bobbleheads have no price in the data.

## Overview

The table below lists each vehicle skin sold on the Gold Market, sorted by vehicle. The MH-6 (Little Bird) has three skins, one in each set, so it appears three times.

## Vehicle skins

| Vehicle | Skin set | Season | Price (Gold Bars) | How it unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bobcat | Two-Tone Desert | Season 1 | 5 | Gold Market |
| Dune Buggy | Two-Tone Desert | Season 1 | 10 | Gold Market |
| Havoc | Two-Tone Desert | Season 2 | 15 | Gold Market |
| Humvee | Two-Tone Desert | Season 1 | 10 | Gold Market |
| MH-6 | Black and Gold | Season 2 | 30 | Gold Market (see the note below) |
| MH-6 | Two-Tone Desert | Season 1 | 15 | Gold Market |
| MH-6 | Taxi | Not stated | Not sold | Not yet known |

The Gold Market prices are 6 vehicle skins worth 85 Gold Bars in total.

The MH-6 Black and Gold skin is the most expensive vehicle skin, at 30 Gold Bars. Its Gold Market entry is a single hero tile, and it carries an expiry timestamp of 31 December 2026 in the data. The data also marks that entry as having no end time, so whether it leaves the market at that date is not yet known.

## Taxi skin

The taxi skin is for the MH-6 (Little Bird). It is the only taxi cosmetic. It works by swapping materials on the helicopter rather than changing its shape. The list of material swaps is in the data but is not expanded here. The data gives the taxi skin no price and no Gold Market row, so how a player gets it is not yet known.

## Bobbleheads

Bobbleheads are vehicle cosmetics that fit a vehicle slot. The data has four of them. None has a Gold Market price, and the data does not say how they are unlocked.

| Bobblehead | Price (Gold Bars) | How it unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| Faction | Not sold | Not yet known |
| Grenade | Not sold | Not yet known |
| Helmet | Not sold | Not yet known |
| A fourth bobblehead | Not sold | Not yet known (the data gives it no display name) |

The data gives the bobbleheads no vehicle filter, so they are not limited to particular vehicles.

## Cost and unlocking

- **Gold Market.** Every priced vehicle skin is a Gold Market entry, bought with Gold Bars. Gold Bars are covered in [Currencies](currencies.md).
- **Look on other vehicles.** Digital Wood and Chocolate Chip also set how their looks appear on other vehicles, such as the Kodiak, Ural and Humvee variants. The data does not list those as sold skins, and it does not say whether a player can apply them.
- **No level gate.** The data does not tie any vehicle skin to a level. See [Season 1 Cosmetics](season-01-cosmetics.md).

## Season 2 changes

- The Havoc attack helicopter gets a Two-Tone Desert skin for 15 Gold Bars.
- The Little Bird (MH-6) gets a Black and Gold skin for 30 Gold Bars.
