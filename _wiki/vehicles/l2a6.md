The L2A6 is the heavy tank of the tank family. It has a 120×570 mm cannon, a coaxial machine gun and a mounted machine gun, and it has a tougher hull zone than the other tanks in the family.

## Overview

The L2A6 is sold in the vehicle vendor under land tank, for 22,000 cash, and unlocks at level 1. Its Season 2 price went up from 14,000.

## Armor and damage

Main health is 800 HP. The L2A6 has separate health for its engine (150 HP), fuel tank (150 HP), each track (125 HP) and a strong zone on the hull (350 HP).

The hull's strong zone sends 40% of the damage from a hit there to the main health while the zone stands. The zone takes the rest. When the zone is destroyed, the remaining damage goes to the main health.

Rifle rounds do no damage, so rifle fire cannot destroy it. Cannon rounds do work:

| Ammunition | Hits to destroy |
|---|---:|
| 5.56 mm, 7.62×54 mm, .308 Win, .50 cal | Cannot be destroyed |
| 20×102 mm cannon | 312 |
| 30 mm cannon | 47 |

## Handling

- Tracked drive with a top speed of 65 and a maximum incline of 50 degrees.
- Track rebound damping is shared across the tank family.

## Season 2 changes

- Price rose from 14,000 to 22,000 cash.
- A new 125 mm rocket rule adds +31.5%.
- Two interaction points on the sides of the tank moved outward, from an offset of 40 to 85 (units not stated).
- Track rebound damping rose from 12,000 to 17,000 across the tank family.
- The vehicle item is wider in inventory (width 4 to 6).
