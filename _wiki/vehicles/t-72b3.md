The T-72B3 is a heavy tracked tank added in Season 2. Its main gun is a 125 mm cannon, with a coaxial machine gun and a separate mounted machine gun.

## Overview

The T-72B3 is sold in the vehicle vendor under land tank, for 16,000 cash, and unlocks at level 1. It was added in Season 2, so the Season 1 data does not include it.

The data does not model any reactive armor on this tank. Its protection comes from the damage rules by damage type, not from separate armor pieces.

## Armor and damage

Main health is 750 HP. The T-72B3 has separate health for its engine (150 HP), fuel tank (150 HP) and each track (125 HP).

Rifle rounds do no damage, so rifle fire cannot destroy it. Cannon rounds do work:

| Ammunition | Hits to destroy |
|---|---:|
| 5.56 mm, 7.62×54 mm, .308 Win, .50 cal | Cannot be destroyed |
| 20×102 mm cannon | 176 |
| 30 mm cannon | 38 |
