The T-72B3 is a heavy tracked tank added in Season 2. Its main gun is a 125 mm cannon, with a coaxial machine gun and a separate mounted machine gun.

## Overview

The T-72B3 is sold in the vehicle vendor under land tank, for 16,000 cash, and unlocks at level 1. It was added in Season 2, so the Season 1 data does not include it.

The data does not model any reactive armor on this tank. Its protection comes from the damage rules by damage type, not from separate armor pieces.

## Armor and damage

Main health is 750 HP. The T-72B3 has separate health for its engine (150 HP), fuel tank (150 HP) and each track (125 HP).

Rifle rounds do no damage, so rifle fire cannot destroy it. Cannon rounds do work:

| Ammunition | Hits to destroy |
|---|---:|
| 5.56 mm, 7.62×54 mm, .308 Win, .50 cal | Cannot be destroyed |
| 20×102 mm cannon | 176 |
| 30 mm cannon | 38 |

## Weapons and seats

| Weapon | Type | Rate | Effective range | Reload | Starting ammunition |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2A46M-5 Cannon (125 mm) | Cannon | 50 rounds/min | 10,000 m | 4.0 s | 9 |
| PKTM Coaxial Gun (7.62 mm class) | Machine gun | 600 rounds/min (default) | 1,000 m | 4.25 s | 300 (tracer) |
| M2 Browning Machine Gun (mounted) | Machine gun | 600 rounds/min (default) | 1,000 m | 8.0 s | 0 (tracer) |

The three seats are two in the hull, the driver and the gunner of the mounted machine gun, and one turret gunner. The T-72B3 also carries a smoke countermeasure with one charge. Its smoke screen stays active for 16 seconds, and the T-72B3 can use smoke again 90 seconds after the first use. The data lists one starting charge. Smoke charges follow the vehicle inventory in the same way as flares: the smoke item must be added to the vehicle. The vehicle vendor sells smoke for 750 cash, one charge per purchase, the same as flares.

## Handling

- Tracked drive with a top speed of 60 and a maximum incline of 50 degrees.

**Engine torque.** The torque curve is set against engine RPM up to 5,000 in the tracked drive data, and the torque values carry no unit label. See [Vehicle handling](vehicle-handling.md) for the other tracked numbers.

## Season 2 changes

- Added in Season 2 with a price of 16,000 cash.

## Vehicle spawns

The T-72B3 is placed by the ground vehicle yards of all four main maps: [Bakurani](../game-modes-and-maps/bakurani.md), [Ozeti](../game-modes-and-maps/ozeti.md), [Zestafona](../game-modes-and-maps/zestafona.md) and [Saburtulo](../game-modes-and-maps/saburtulo.md). The yards list the vehicle family, not each variant, so which variant appears in a slot is not in the data (inferred). A match offers it in 7 of the 7 ground yards on Ozeti, 5 or 6 of the 7 or 6 on Bakurani, 7 of 7 on Zestafona and 6 of 6 on Saburtulo. The data gives no vehicle count or respawn timer per yard. See [Vehicle Spawns](../../game-features/Wardogs_VehicleSpawns_v1.md).
