The Ural Defender is the protected version of the [Ural](ural.md). The shop describes it as a large passenger vehicle with protection and multiple capacity. It has no weapons.

## Overview

The Ural Defender is sold in the vehicle vendor under land transport, for 7,200 cash, and unlocks at level 0. The data gives it the same stats sheet, handling and damage numbers as the base Ural. The shop text describes protection, but the data does not show a difference in the numbers for this variant.

## Armor and damage

Main health is 700 HP, with the same engine, fuel tank and wheel health as the [Ural](ural.md). Its destruction counts match the base Ural:

| Ammunition | Hits to destroy | Faster route |
|---|---:|---|
| 5.56 mm rifle | 1,000 | 175 through the fuel tank |
| 7.62×54 mm rifle | 637 | 112 through the fuel tank |
| .308 Win | 1,778 | 311 through the fuel tank |
| .50 cal | 312 | 55 through the fuel tank |
| 20×102 mm cannon | 94 | None found |
| 30 mm cannon | 12 | None found |

## Weapons and seats

The Ural Defender has no weapons. Its three seats are a driver and two passengers.

## Handling

Handling matches the base Ural: mass 10,000, hand brake 50, front steering of 30 degrees, and the same six-speed gearbox.

## Season 2 changes

- Price rose from 6,000 to 7,200 cash.
- The Ural damage rules that apply to this variant changed as listed on the [Ural](ural.md) article.
