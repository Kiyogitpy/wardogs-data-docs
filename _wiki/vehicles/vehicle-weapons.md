This page lists every gun mounted on a vehicle in Wardogs, from the miniguns on the helicopters to the cannons on the tanks and the SPH-2 artillery gun. Each row links to the vehicle article that carries the weapon. Guns on buildable emplacements are not on this page; they are in the emplacement articles listed under [Buildables](../equipment/buildables.md).

## Miniguns

The M134D minigun is the same gun on three vehicles, and it fires .308 Win rounds at 1,500 rounds/min everywhere. The AH-6M and the Humvee [Minigun] reach 800 m and reload in 4.25 s. The Z20 Lakota [Miniguns] reaches 1,500 m and reloads in 5.0 s. Each starts with 150 or 300 tracer rounds, as the table shows. The data does not say which seat fires the miniguns on the helicopter and the Humvee.

Season 2 changed the damage table entries of the AH-6M and the Humvee [Minigun], and patch notes describe calibre reductions for these miniguns. The [Vehicles changes page](../../season-2-changes/vehicles.md) lists the damage changes.

## Machine guns

The M249 appears on four vehicles, and all four use 5.56 mm rounds. The data gives a 4.25 s reload for each. The articles for the Humvee, the Kodiak and the Ural Defender list the rate, range and starting ammunition as not yet known, and the L2A6 article gives the same for its rate and range.

The M2 Browning is a .50 cal gun on two vehicles. The Wolf-110 ring turret and the T-72B3 mounted gun both have a data reload of 8.0 s. The T-72B3 article gives this gun's reload as not yet known.

The L2A6 has a coaxial MG3A1 that fires .308 Win rounds at 650 rounds/min to 200 m. The T-72B3 has a coaxial PKTM, a 7.62 mm class gun, with a 4.25 s reload and 300 tracer rounds; its rate and range are not yet known.

Season 2 raised the gamepad rotation sensitivity for the ring turrets of the Kodiak [M249], the Humvee and the Ural Defender from 35 to 80, and for the L2A6's mounted gun as well.

## Rocket pods

The B-13 is a 122 mm rocket pod. The Havoc carries it at 500 rounds/min with a 6.0 s reload and a 1,000 m range. The AH-6R [Rockets] carries two pods at 350 rounds/min, also with a 1,000 m range. The AH-6R article does not give its reload or starting rocket count, and the data gives a 6.0 s reload for the same pod.

Season 2 changed one Havoc damage table entry, from 87.5 to -46.0, as the [Vehicles changes page](../../season-2-changes/vehicles.md) shows.

## Autocannons and anti-air cannons

The Havoc's 2A42 autocannon fires 30 mm rounds at 250 rounds/min with an effective range of 600 m and an 8.0 s reload. It has no starting rounds in the article.

The Flakpanzer Gepard carries twin Duel 35 mm Oerlikon GDF cannons. They fire at 1,100 rounds/min with a 1,000 m range and a 4.0 s reload. The article records no starting rounds.

## Tank cannons

The L2A6 and the T-72B3 both carry a main gun that fires at 50 rounds/min with a range of 10,000 m and a 4.0 s reload. The L2A6's L55A1 fires 120x570 mm rounds, and the T-72B3's 2A46M-5 fires 125 mm rounds. Each starts with 9 rounds.

Season 2 changed one damage table entry for the L2A6, from -60 to -84, and added a 125 mm rocket entry of -10. The [Vehicles changes page](../../season-2-changes/vehicles.md) has the full list.

## Artillery

The SPH-2's L52 fires 155 mm shells at 50 rounds/min with a 10,000 m range. The reload is 25.0 s, the longest in this table. It starts with 10 Expensive shells, which have a camera that follows the round in flight, and 0 Standard shells. The [Artillery](../game-mechanics/artillery.md) page explains how the gun fires and aims.
