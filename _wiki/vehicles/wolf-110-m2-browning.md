The Wolf-110 [M2 Browning] is the [Wolf-110](wolf-110.md) with an M2 Browning machine gun on a ring turret. It seats five and was added in Season 2.

## Overview

The Wolf-110 [M2 Browning] is sold in the vehicle vendor under land transport, for 7,800 cash, and unlocks at level 0. It was added in Season 2.

## Armor and damage

Main health is 500 HP, with the same engine and fuel tank health as the [Wolf-110](wolf-110.md). Rifle rounds do 4.5% of their damage against the hull.

The data simulates this variant with the same destruction numbers as the base Wolf-110, so the hits to destroy are the same:

| Ammunition | Hits to destroy | Faster route |
|---|---:|---|
| 5.56 mm rifle | 715 | 204 through the left front wheel |
| 7.62×54 mm rifle | 455 | 130 through the left front wheel |
| .308 Win | 889 | 254 through the left front wheel |
| .50 cal | 223 | 63 through the left front wheel |
| 20×102 mm cannon | 53 | None found |
| 30 mm cannon | 13 | None found |

## Weapons and seats

| Weapon | Type | Rate | Effective range | Reload | Starting ammunition |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|

The data does not give the rate of fire, range, reload or starting ammunition for this gun. The five seats are a driver, three active passengers and a gunner on the ring turret.

## Handling

Handling matches the base Wolf-110: mass 3,200, hand brake 80, front steering of 35 degrees, and the same torque split.

## Season 2 changes

- Added in Season 2 with a price of 7,800 cash.
- Season 2 also added steering and free-look curves for the Wolf-110 family.
