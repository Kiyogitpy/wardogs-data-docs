The Z20 Lakota [Miniguns] is the armed version of the [Z20 Lakota](z20-lakota.md). It has the same large airframe with two M134D miniguns added, so it can fight and still carry many passengers.

## Overview

The armed Z20 is sold in the vehicle vendor under air combat, for 15,000 cash. It unlocks at level 0. It shares the health, speed and damage numbers of the transport model.

The shop description says 11 passengers, which is fewer than the 13 seats in the vendor stats.

## Armor and damage

Main health is 600 HP. The engine has 200 HP, the fuel tank 200 HP, the main rotor 400 HP and the tail rotor 300 HP.

Rifle damage works as on the [Z20 Lakota](z20-lakota.md). The tail rotor is the weak point: 429 rifle hits there destroy the helicopter, against 858 hits to the body.

## Weapons and seats

| Weapon | Type | Rate | Effective range | Reload | Starting ammunition |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| M134D Minigun (two) | Machine gun | 1,500 rounds/min | 1,500 m | 5.0 s | 150 (tracer) |

The two miniguns are fired by two dedicated gunner seats, one for the left gun and one for the right gun. The pilot and the other passengers do not fire them. Each gunner takes the gun automatically on entering the seat. The other eleven seats are the pilot and passenger seats of the standard Z20 Lakota.

## Season 2 changes

- Price rose from 8,000 to 15,000 cash.
- The 35×228 mm cannon rule changed from -40% to -57.25%.
- A new 125 mm rocket rule adds +150%.
- The vehicle item is one step wider in inventory (width 4 to 5).
- The flares button is renamed Fire Countermeasures. The key is the same (V on keyboard).

## Vehicle spawns

The Z20 Lakota [Miniguns] is covered by the air vehicle yards (inferred). Each side normally has one air yard, and every yard allows any helicopter by a general air tag, so it can be placed on all four main maps (three air yards per match). The Z20 Lakota family (with and without miniguns) is named directly in one Zestafona yard, the extra air yard of the Lonestar side. The airframe is not named in those yards, so which helicopter appears in a slot is not known. See [Vehicle Spawns](../../game-features/Wardogs_VehicleSpawns_v1.md).
