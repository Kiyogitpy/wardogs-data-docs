The AK74 is an assault rifle chambered in 5.45x39mm. It fires fully automatic and semi-automatic at 650 rounds/min. Its standard magazine is the AK74 30 RND Magazine.

## Overview

Assault rifles are the AR class in the weapon data.

It takes the bullet types described on the [Ammunition](ammunition.md) page: Standard (FMJ), Flesh Damage (HP), Armor Piercing (AP). Each has a tracer version.

## Recoil and spread

Each bullet leaves the barrel in a random direction inside a cone around the point you aim at. Hip fire uses a wide cone that grows when you move. Aiming down the sights uses a very narrow one. Every shot also pushes the view up and to the side, following the weapon's recoil pattern.

**Spread**

| Situation | Still | Moving at full speed |
|---|---|---|
| Standing, hip fire | 3.0° | 6.0° |
| Crouching, hip fire | 2.0° | 4.0° |
| Prone, hip fire | 1.5° | 3.0° |
| In the air, hip fire | 10.0° | 10.0° |
| Aimed down sights | 0.0236° | 0.0236° |

Cone half-angles in degrees: a bullet can land up to that far from the aim point. At 100 m, standing hip fire has a radius of 5.2 m when still and 10.5 m at full speed; aimed, it is 0.04 m. Half of the shots land within half that radius.

Accuracy is 0.825 mrad, a full cone angle; the aimed row is half of it, in degrees.

- Shooting does not widen the spread. The data has no per-shot bloom and no recovery timer.
- Hip spread moves with speed, stance and being in the air, and eases to its new value in about 0.04 s.
- Fully aimed, hip spread drops to zero at once, so only the aimed row applies.
- The figures are before attachments, which can change spread (see [Attachments](attachments.md)).
- Between still and full speed, hip spread is a straight line.

**Recoil**

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Shots in the recoil pattern | 30 |
| Kick on the first shot | 0.72° up |
| Climb after 10 shots | 11.1° up |
| Climb after 30 shots | 32.5° up |
| Average climb per shot over 30 shots | 1.08° up |
| Sideways drift after 30 shots | 0.6° right |
| Pattern resets after | 0.65 s without firing |
| Vertical kick crouching / prone, compared with standing | 0.85× / 0.70× |
| Default-loadout recoil multiplier, vertical / horizontal | 1 / 1 |

Climb and drift figures are computed from the pattern points, before the recoil multiplier. The game turns each authored kick by up to 13° per shot, so the real sideways drift varies from burst to burst. After shot 30 each kick is random inside the post-pattern range.

Season 2 did not change the recoil pattern.

To see the whole pattern as a picture, open the [Weapon Spray Viewer](https://kiyogitpy.github.io/wardogs-data-docs/pipelines/spray-viewer.html) and pick the AK74 in the viewer.
