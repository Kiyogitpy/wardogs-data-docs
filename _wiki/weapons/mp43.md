The MP43 is a shotgun chambered in 12 gauge. It fires semi-automatic at 900 rounds/min.

## Overview

Shotguns are the shotgun class in the weapon data. They fire shells rather than single bullets.

It takes the shells described on the [Ammunition](../ammunition.md) page: Buckshot, Rifled slug. Each has a tracer version.

## Recoil and spread

Each bullet leaves the barrel in a random direction inside a cone around the point you aim at. Hip fire uses a wide cone that grows when you move. Aiming down the sights uses a very narrow one. Every shot also pushes the view up and to the side, following the weapon's recoil pattern.

**Spread**

| Situation | Still | Moving at full speed |
|---|---|---|
| Standing, hip fire | 3.0° | 6.0° |
| Crouching, hip fire | 2.0° | 4.0° |
| Prone, hip fire | 1.0° | 2.0° |
| In the air, hip fire | 10.0° | 10.0° |
| Aimed down sights | 0.0882° | 0.0882° |

Cone half-angles in degrees: a bullet can land up to that far from the aim point. At 100 m, standing hip fire has a radius of 5.2 m when still and 10.5 m at full speed; aimed, it is 0.15 m. Half of the shots land within half that radius.

Accuracy is 3.08 mrad, a full cone angle; the aimed row is half of it, in degrees.

Buckshot fires several pellets per shot. For a multi-pellet round the hip spread rows above are not used; the pellet pattern comes from the weapon's shotgun data instead (see [Weapon Spread pipeline](../../pipelines/Wardogs_WeaponSpread_v1.md)). The hip rows apply to single-projectile ammunition such as the rifled slug.

- The data has no per-shot bloom and no recovery timer: nothing found in the spread data makes firing widen the spread, and spread changes only with speed, stance, being in the air and aiming. This comes from reading the game files, not from testing in game.
- Hip spread eases toward its new value with a time constant of about 0.04 s (a native default found by static reading).
- The 10° in-air figure is a native default found by static reading; only the grenade launcher's data sets it, to the same 10°.
- At full aim (aim blend exactly 1.0) hip spread is set to zero, so only the aimed row applies. A partly aimed weapon counts as hip fire.
- "Full speed" means 4 m/s standing, 2.5 m/s crouching and 0.5 m/s prone; between still and that speed the hip spread is a straight line.
- The figures are before attachments, which can change spread (see [Attachments](../attachments.md)).

**Recoil**

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Shots in the recoil pattern | 2 |
| Kick on the first shot | 1.71° up |
| Climb after 10 shots | 12.4° up |
| Climb after 30 shots | 34.4° up |
| Average climb per shot over 30 shots | 1.15° up |
| Sideways drift after 30 shots | 1.3° right |
| Pattern resets after | 1.2 s without firing |
| Vertical kick crouching / prone, compared with standing | 0.85× / 0.70× |
| Default-loadout recoil multiplier, vertical / horizontal | 1 / 1 |

Climb and drift figures are computed from the pattern points, before the recoil multiplier. The game turns each authored kick by up to 28° per shot, so the real sideways drift varies from burst to burst. After shot 2 each kick is random inside the post-pattern range.

Season 2 did not change the recoil pattern.

To see the whole pattern as a picture, open the [Weapon Spray Viewer](https://kiyogitpy.github.io/wardogs-data-docs/pipelines/spray-viewer.html) and pick the MP43 in the viewer.
