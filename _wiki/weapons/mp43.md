The MP43 is a shotgun chambered in 12g. It fires semi-automatic at 900 rounds/min.

## Overview

Shotguns are the shotgun class in the weapon data. They fire shells rather than single bullets.

It takes the shells described on the [Ammunition](ammunition.md) page: Buckshot, Rifled slug. Each has a tracer version.

## Damage

Figures are for Buckshot at point blank range, per pellet (8 pellets per shot), against a character without armor, before any falloff.

|---|---|

| Upper torso | 24.8 |
| Middle torso | 22.5 |
| Upper arm | 15.8 |
| Thigh | 15.8 |
