The game data lists an underbarrel launcher named "TEST Grenade Launcher" that fires 40 mm explosive rounds. The name reads as a test or placeholder item. It has a full set of stats, but no shop price or unlock level appears for it, and whether players can get or use it in a live match is not confirmed. It is a mountable attachment rather than a standalone weapon, so it is not in the weapon list.

## Overview

The launcher fires one 40 mm round per shot in semi-automatic mode at 75 rounds/min. Each shot uses one 40 mm round from the player's inventory.

## Damage

Each round is an explosion with a 100 damage base. The data gives an effective radius of 2.5 m and an outer radius of 10 m, with the damage falling off between them. The round also deals 80 blunt damage and an impulse that pushes what it hits. The round arms 0.07 s after it leaves the barrel.

## Ammunition

The launcher uses 40 mm rounds, one per shot.

## Cost and unlocking

The item does not appear in the shop data, and no unlock level for it is listed.
