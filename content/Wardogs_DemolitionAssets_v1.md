# Wardogs: Demolition Assets (every file under [`Content/Demolition`](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/tree/main/data/Wardogs/Content/Demolition), row level)

*Source: all 86 files of [`Wardogs/Content/Demolition`](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/tree/main/data/Wardogs/Content/Demolition) (39 demolition data assets, 9 component settings, 19 remnant-mesh assets, 19 rulesets), plus the seven `AE_PropDemolition_*` audio events that carry the crosstalk tags. Every table below is generated by [`_tools/demolition_tables.py`](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/blob/main/_tools/demolition_tables.py); do not edit it by hand.*

This page is the full-name, row-level companion to the [Demolition reference](Wardogs_Demolition_v2.md), which keeps the Season 02 change log, the abbreviated per-asset summary and the notes. Asset names here keep their `DA_Demolition_` / `BH_Demolition_RS_` prefixes; inside table cells the `DA_Demolition_` prefix is dropped to keep rows short.

Related pages: [Buildables](../game-features/Wardogs_Buildables_v1.md) (the [`Buildables`](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/blob/main/data/Wardogs/Plugins/GameFeatures/Items/Buildables/Content/Buildables.json) ruleset lets placed structures demolish props), [Explosives](../game-features/Wardogs_Explosives_v1.md) (the damage-source tags the explosion rules list), [Fire and Utility](../game-features/Wardogs_FireAndUtility_v1.md) (Molotov), [Destruction](Wardogs_Destruction_v2.md) (building destruction, a separate system) and [Vehicle Handling](../game-features/Wardogs_VehicleHandling_v1.md) (the vehicle variants the vehicle rules name).

## 1. What each asset type is

| Asset type | What the export holds | What it does not hold |
|---|---|---|
| `BHDemolitionDataAsset` (`DA_Demolition_*`) | One destructible prop family: a map of source static meshes to far-representation mesh, remnant set and colour data; one component-settings asset; one ruleset; permitted zones; a Niagara system; a crosstalk audio tag; fragment restitution; optionally a remnant actor | No health, damage threshold, fragment count, mass or break impulse |
| `BHDemolitionComponentSettings` (`DA_Demolition_CS_*`, [`DA_CS_Saplings`](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/blob/main/data/Wardogs/Content/Demolition/ComponentSettings/DA_CS_Saplings.json)) | Instance cull distances, ray-tracing flag, shadow-cache mode, Nanite and WPO distances, collision profile | - |
| `BHDemolitionRemnantMeshesAsset` (`DA_Demolition_RM_*`) | Near and far static mesh of what is left standing after the break | Offsets and overrides (null in every entry) |
| `BHDemolitionRuleset` (`BH_Demolition_RS_*`) | A tree of rules: explosion damage-source tags with a minimum distance, vehicle variants, a buildable rule, or an OR of other rulesets | The trigger logic that evaluates them |

Where a "what it means" statement appears below, it is **inference** from names and value ranges unless the export states it.

## 9. Who references these assets

- **Within `data/`:** nothing outside the folder references a demolition data asset, a remnant set or a ruleset. The only outside hit is the developer copy of `DA_Demolition_CS_Default` (a copy by name, not a reference). The [`Buildables`](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/blob/main/data/Wardogs/Plugins/GameFeatures/Items/Buildables/Content/Buildables.json) ruleset names no buildable item; it is a single `WDDemolitionRule_Buildable` with no properties (see [Buildables](../game-features/Wardogs_Buildables_v1.md)).
- **Not in `data/`:** the level actors or instanced meshes that carry a `BHDemolitionDataAsset` (maps are not part of the export), and the code that evaluates the rules. That a prop in a level is "demolishable" is therefore only visible through the source-mesh names in section 4, which name the intact mesh.
- **Referenced by the demolition assets:** component settings (section 5), remnant sets (section 6), rulesets (section 7), Niagara systems and audio tags (section 8), and the remnant actor `BP_Remnant_RoadSigns_C` on the two signage assets.

## 10. Skipped and not pulled

- **Skipped as audio/VFX:** the Niagara systems named in section 8 (`NS_*Smash*`), the seven `AE_PropDemolition_*` audio events, and the source, far-representation and remnant static meshes themselves (referenced by name only; mesh geometry and materials are outside this table scope). `BP_Remnant_RoadSigns_C` is named but its contents are not documented.
- **Not in the export:** damage-to-break thresholds, fragment counts and mass, the meaning of `Demolition.Zone.Important` / `Distant` (read as level-of-detail style zones by name; **inference**), and units for the cull and minimum distances (centimetres is the likely unit; not confirmed, see [`docs/UNITS.md`](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/blob/main/docs/UNITS.md)).
