# Wardogs: Explosion, Impact, Prop and Muzzle Flash Fields

*Source: the data assets under [`Content/VFX/Explosions`](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/tree/main/data/Wardogs/Content/VFX/Explosions), [`Content/VFX/Weapons/Impacts`](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/tree/main/data/Wardogs/Content/VFX/Weapons/Impacts), [`Content/VFX/Weapons/MuzzleFlashes`](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/tree/main/data/Wardogs/Content/VFX/Weapons/MuzzleFlashes) and [`Content/Environment/Blueprints/VehiclePropTooling`](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/tree/main/data/Wardogs/Content/Environment/Blueprints/VehiclePropTooling). The tables are generated by [`_tools/fx_data_tables.py`](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/blob/main/_tools/fx_data_tables.py) and checked with `--check`. Evidence: [round 89 env](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/blob/main/docs/evidence/round89-env.md).*

This page lists every field the assets store, so it repeats some columns of [VFX](Wardogs_VFX_v2.md) (the shorter explosion and impact tables) and [Muzzle Flashes](Wardogs_MuzzleFlashes_v1.md) (the flash tables with notes on how the layers fit). A blank cell means the asset leaves the field unset and the class default applies; the export does not give that default. The units of sizes, distances and amounts are not stated in the export. Misspellings such as `Multiplyer` and `CaliberSIze` are the game's own names.

## 4. Muzzle flashes

A weapon recipe (`PDA_MuzzleFlash_C`) names its default muzzle device in the last column, and the device asset (`PDA_MuzzleFlashAttachement_C`) holds multipliers. Recipe names follow the weapon, for example [`DA_MuzzleFlash_AK74-M`](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/blob/main/data/Wardogs/Content/VFX/Weapons/MuzzleFlashes/MuzzleFlashData/AssualtRifles/DA_MuzzleFlash_AK74-M.json) uses [`DA_MF_AK74-M_Default`](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/blob/main/data/Wardogs/Content/VFX/Weapons/MuzzleFlashes/AttachmentMuzzleFlashData/DA_MF_AK74-M_Default.json). The export does not store which shop muzzle device uses which device asset; see [Muzzle Flashes](Wardogs_MuzzleFlashes_v1.md). Colours are red / green / blue as stored; the values go above 1 because they are HDR emissive colours, and the `Hex` field the export prints next to them does not match them, so it is left out.
