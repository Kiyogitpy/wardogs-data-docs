# Wardogs: Muzzle Flashes (ammo, muzzle device and weapon recipes)

*Source: the 107 assets under [`Content/VFX/Weapons/MuzzleFlashes`](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/tree/main/data/Wardogs/Content/VFX/Weapons/MuzzleFlashes) in three folders. Folder-level counts are in [VFX](Wardogs_VFX_v2.md); this page lists the values. The tables are generated by `_tools/muzzle_flash_tables.py` and checked with `--check`. Misspellings such as `Multiplyer` and `AssualtRifles` are the game's own.*

The full field set of every recipe and device asset (colours, gas, sparks, lights, pattern values) is on [Explosion, Impact, Prop and Flash Fields](Wardogs_FX_Field_Tables_v1.md#4-muzzle-flashes).

## 2. How the three layers fit

- A **weapon recipe** (`PDA_MuzzleFlash_C`, section 5) holds the base flash: size seen from the first person camera (1P) and from other players (3P), lifetime in seconds, a distant-flash size and its maximum, and a `DefaultMuzzle` that names one muzzle-device asset.
- A **muzzle-device asset** (`PDA_MuzzleFlashAttachement_C`, section 4) holds multipliers. Most factory muzzle assets (`*_DefaultMuzzle`) use 1.5 for the 1P size and a light multiplier of 0.1. The suppressor and flash hider assets use 0.3 to 0.7 for the 1P size, 0.5 to 0.7 for the 3P size and 0.05 for light.
- An **ammo asset** (`PDA_MuzzleFlashAmmuniton_C`, section 3) can override the flash colour and the sparks.
- The export does not store which asset a given shop muzzle device uses, nor the rule that combines the layers. The multiplication of recipe size by device multiplier is the natural reading of the field names, not something read from a function. Of the 33 device assets that name a mesh, 31 point at the same suppressor mesh `SK_MZL_04` (one at `SK_MZL_05`, one at `SK_PWSCQB74_Muzzle`), so the mesh is a shared visual and does not identify the shop item.
- All numbers are cooked defaults. The flash size units are not stated in the export.
