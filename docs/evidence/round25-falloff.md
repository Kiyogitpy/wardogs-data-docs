# Round 25: damage over distance, curve and speed scale

Generated by `python _tools/weapon_damage_falloff.py --json` into `assets/data/weapon-damage-falloff.json`.
`--check` verifies it against `damage_tables.py --falloff` and `assets/data/weapon-damage.json`. `--rank AMMO ARMOR LOCATION DISTANCE_M` ranks weapons at one of the 14 stored distances. `--sensitivity` prints the speed comparisons quoted below.

## Inputs

- The point-blank engine `_tools/weapon_damage_tables.py` (33 weapons, 190 weapon x ammo rows, torso and head, five armor classes each).
- Each weapon's stats asset: `DistanceDamageFalloffCurve` (keys in centimetres), `bUseDistanceDamageFalloffCurve`, `InitialBulletSpeed`, `bOverrideTypicalSpeed`, `TypicalSpeed`, `EffectiveRange`.
- The projectile data asset for each ammo row (the one `damage_tables.projectiles()` picks): `TypicalSpeed` and `ProjectileDimensions` (Length, Diameter, Mass, DragModel value and curve row).
- `CT_Projectile_DragCoefficients` (every row linear, constant extrapolation).
- The drag formula from Projectile Flight section 5 and the speed and distance scales from Damage Pipeline v2 section 7.6.

## Formulas

damage(d) = point-blank damage x curve(d) x speed_scale(d). Point-blank damage already holds every ammo and armor rule. The rule chain is a product of factors on the starting amount (`damage_tables.evaluate`), so scaling the amount scales the result. `damage_tables.py --falloff` makes the same assumption; it was not tested against the game.

Curve. Unreal `FRichCurve::Eval`: constant before the first key and after the last key (the weapon curves name no extrapolation mode; the drag curves name RCCE_Constant, which is also the engine default). Between two keys the segment takes the interp mode of its first key. Linear is a lerp, constant holds the first key's value, cubic is a Hermite spline with tangents `LeaveTangent * dt` of the first key and `ArriveTangent * dt` of the second, in value per centimetre. Every key has `RCTWM_WeightedNone`, so tangent weights do not enter.

Speed scale. `clamp(speed / reference, 0, 1)`. The reference is the weapon's `TypicalSpeed` when `bOverrideTypicalSpeed` is set and the value is above 0, otherwise the projectile asset's `TypicalSpeed` (native `0x6C255B0`). So the scale applies to every weapon, not only the 24 overrides that Damage Pipeline section 7.8 counts: weapons without an override are scaled against their projectile's `TypicalSpeed`.

Speed at distance. The documented drag model, integrated with trapezoid steps of 1 ms from `InitialBulletSpeed`: `a_drag = -100 * 0.5 * rho * Cd * (pi/4) * (D/100)^2 * (|v|/100)^2 / m` along the velocity, `rho` 1.2, `Cd = DragModel.Value * Curve(|v| in m/s)`; no wind, no thrust. The shot runs along the line of sight and gravity is left out, because a level shot falls out of reach well before 1200 m, so a distance column would not be defined for it, and a real shooter elevates the barrel.

Sensitivity of that choice (`--sensitivity`, first round of each distinct projectile and muzzle speed, 200 to 1200 m):

- Gravity. Solving the elevation that lands the shot at the muzzle height (gravity -980 cm/s^2, 4 ms steps) changes the speed scale by under 1 percent at 200 m and 400 m for rifles. The largest relative difference found is 22 percent, for the 9mm GGX 17 at 1200 m (scale 0.18 on the line of sight, 0.22 elevated, since the descending bullet is faster).
- Step length. The game steps by `floor(1000 / Rate)` ms and Rate was not read (Projectile Flight open item 3). With 16 ms steps the speed scale differs from the 1 ms result by at most 2.2 percent (M500 buckshot at 800 m).

## Exactness of each curve

30 of 33 weapons apply a distance curve. Judge has none, and M500 and MP43 have a stored curve without the flag (below).

- Linear, exact: 26 weapons. `--check` reproduces every cell of `damage_tables.py --falloff` (50, 100, 200, 400, 800 and 1200 m) for the 29 weapons that table prints without the dagger marker. That count includes Vector (all six Vector keys are RCIM_Linear, so it is not a cubic curve, contrary to the dagger list in the brief) and the three weapons with no applied curve.
- Cubic: WEPN_028 (MP5), MP9, SMG_03 and WEPN_031 (same curve as SMG_03). Every key stores ArriveTangent and LeaveTangent, so the spline is exact and no fallback is used. Key modes: MP9 is linear at 0 m and cubic at 100, 200 and 1200 m; the other three are linear at 0 m, cubic at 200 and 300 m and linear at 1200 m. The segments from 100 m (MP9) or 200 m (the others) to the end are all cubic.
- Linear versus cubic (the dagger rows of `damage_tables.py --falloff` and Damage Pipeline section 7.8), over 0 to 1200 m at 10 cm steps: the largest multiplier difference is 0.218 for MP9 at 608.8 m (exact 0.18, linear 0.40), 0.207 for WEPN_028 at 673 m, and 0.175 for SMG_03 and WEPN_031 at 691.6 m. At 400 m the exact multipliers are 0.442 (WEPN_028), 0.333 (MP9) and 0.462 (SMG_03, WEPN_031) against 0.544, 0.500 and 0.544 linear. At 800 m they are 0.133, 0.115 and 0.158 against 0.322, 0.300 and 0.322. The two evaluations agree at every key. The linear table therefore overstates these four SMGs by 18 percent or more between 400 m and 1000 m (SMG_03 at 400 m is the smallest case; at 800 m the overstatement is above 100 percent for all four), and they are the weakest long-range weapons in the exact table.
- MP9's last key is cubic, and its spline dips just below the final value 0.1 before 1200 m (0.098 at 1000 m), as stored.

Curve ranges. The curve holds its last value beyond its last key. The last key is at 1200 m for 25 weapons, 1500 m for LMG_02, WEPN_036 and SV98, 1600 m for MK22 and 2500 m for SR_04. No weapon with a curve ends before 1200 m. Judge has no `DistanceDamageFalloffCurve`, so its multiplier is 1 at every distance and only the speed scale lowers it. M500 and MP43 store a three-key linear curve (1 up to 70 m, 0.2 at 1200 m) but do not set `bUseDistanceDamageFalloffCurve`, so the curve is not applied, as in `damage_tables.py`. The JSON marks `curve.used` false and keeps the keys. I did not read the native default of that flag, so whether the game applies the stored shotgun curve is not determined.

`EffectiveRange` is stored per weapon in metres (null where the stats omit it) for information. No damage code read so far uses it.

Reading it. The A91 is at 0.78 of its curve value at 500 m, 0.64 at 800 m and 0.51 at 1200 m. 9mm and 45 pistols and SMGs are at about 0.4 to 0.6 at 500 to 600 m. The heavy rifle rounds are untouched below about 900 to 1100 m (SV98 905 m, MK22 1030 m, SVDM 1047 m, WEPN_017 1085 m, LMG_02 1141 m). SR_04 stays at 1.0 until 2016 m, beyond every stored distance. The scale separates weapons that share a curve: A91 and TAR21 have the same curve but different muzzle speeds and references.

The muzzle speed is above the reference speed for every row, so the scale is 1 at 0 m and the 0 m column equals `weapon-damage.json` (asserted by `--check`).

## Not modelled or not determined

- Ammo modifiers. Native `0x6BBAB70` multiplies stats fields, including `TypicalSpeed`, by a modifier value, and `GetProjectileStats(AmmoModifierTag)` can return a per-ammo variant. The values are not in the data export, so Tracer, HollowPoint and ArmorPiercing rows use the base projectile asset and carry `modifier_stats_note`. Plain FMJ, Buckshot and Slug rows are not affected.
- `not_reached_in_model`. The model stops a flight at 12 s, my cap, because the bullets' `MaxProjectileLifetime` was not found in the data. In 12 s the 9mm and 45 rounds reach about 1285 m and 1318 m and the 7mm buckshot pellets about 1065 m. The 1500 m cells for those rows, and the 1200 m cells for M500 and MP43 buckshot, have damage null and status `not_reached_in_model`. No row is `derived_no_drag`: every projectile asset has Length, Diameter, Mass and a linear drag row.
- Wind, spread, pellet separation, ricochet, penetration, target height and the actual elevation of a shot are not modelled. Distances are measured from the muzzle along the line of sight.
- Whether the engine applies the speed scale and the distance scale in the order and form read at `0x6B725B0` for armored hits was not retested in game.
