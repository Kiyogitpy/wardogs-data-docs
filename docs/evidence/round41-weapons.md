## Wiki updates

- `_wiki/game-mechanics/ricochets.md` (or the hit-registration article) -> add: a ricochet leaves the surface at a random angle that is usually steeper than a mirror bounce (deflection factor uniform between 0.2 and 10 times the surface-normal speed, mean about 5, and the shallow-angle setting applies to every hit above 1.6 degrees), plus a random cone of up to 40 degrees and a surface-dependent speed loss.
- `_wiki/game-mechanics/hit-registration.md` -> change: "friendly" is decided by the two players' faction tags (same faction means friendly); the "ignore your own vehicle" setting is a plain on/off per round, not a timer; a hit on a soldier is tested against every body part the bullet's path crosses and the best-scoring one counts; the 155 mm shells hit as a 61 cm x 11 cm box, not a point.
- `_wiki/game-mechanics/damage-over-distance.md` (or the weapon articles) -> add the speed scale: damage also falls with the bullet's speed once it drops below the weapon's reference speed (Glock 17 0.80 at 200 m and 0.45 at 600 m; M4 full to 400 m and 0.85 at 600 m; A91 0.91 at 400 m), on top of the distance curve.
- `_wiki/game-mechanics/spread-and-recoil.md` -> change: weapon sway does move the aim in the default camera mode; zero angles are measured per range with the game's own bullet-path trace; there is no spread build-up per shot (no bloom).
- `_wiki/guides/aiming-the-sph-2.md` and `_wiki/game-mechanics/artillery.md` -> add: the dial reads the barrel's pitch against the world horizontal (hull tilt included); the SPH-2 must stabilize (2 s to deploy, 1 s to release, ground tilt under 20 degrees, standing still; it cannot move or fire while changing state) and a heavy hit un-stabilizes it; only the Expensive 155 mm shell can be steered in flight (steering input scale 0.02), the Standard shell cannot.
- `_wiki/weapons/attachments-sights-and-optics.md` -> add: the sight magnification is a multiplier on the weapon and the ADS time comes with the sight; the "zoom" number is the magnification (sway shrinks by it).
- `_wiki/game-mechanics/aim-assist.md` (if present) -> add: four pull modes (all, movement only, look only, off); the RPG has assist off; the other seven weapon classes pull on movement or look input above a 0.1 threshold, by default requiring input.
- `_wiki/game-mechanics/ammo-packing.md` (if present) -> add: time to load, extract or combine rounds is a curve of the round count per caliber, times the magazine's own factor; ammunition variants such as tracer use their parent caliber's curve.
