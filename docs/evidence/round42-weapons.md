## Wiki updates

- `_wiki/game-mechanics/hit-registration.md` -> add: a soldier's calves, forearms and thighs are hit as plain capsules whose radius is the average of their two ends (the tapered shape is not used); a bullet remembers every actor it has already hit and will not hit the same actor again; a shot counts as a hit-scan shot when its ammunition is not a self-moving projectile actor.
- `_wiki/game-mechanics/spread-and-recoil.md` -> add: the spread of vehicle-mounted machine guns eases toward its value at about 25 per second and drops to zero when the gunner is fully aimed down sights; weapons of the plain mounted-weapon class have no native spread.
- `_wiki/game-mechanics/hit-registration.md` (trust note, if wanted) -> add: the server accepts the client's fire message without a check (the validation step always passes) and takes no client-chosen random seed for pellet patterns from it.
