## Team Offence reward extraction

`_tools/zone_rewards.py` could not evaluate the Team Offence rows because that table writes its selector and variable keys with a lower-case first letter (`basePoints`, `causeMultiplier`, `halvingDivisor`) while the expression and `DefaultOption` use `BasePoints`, `CauseMultiplier`, `HalvingDivisor`. The game matches them case-insensitively: `Selectors`, `Variables` and `VariableIndexMap` of `FWDRewardCalculatorExpression` are `TMap<FName, ...>` (`sdk/raw/WDGame_structs.hpp` lines 7932, 7983, 7984) and an FName compares without regard to case (engine semantics; the matching function itself was not read, so this is Derived from the key type). The tool now lower-cases names, evaluates [`DT_ChallengeRewards_TeamOffence`](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/blob/main/data/Wardogs/Plugins/GameFeatures/TeamOffence/Content/Challenges/DT_ChallengeRewards_TeamOffence.json) and writes a `teamOffence` part with each cause context to `assets/data/zone-rewards.json`. `python _tools/zone_rewards.py --check` asserts the values. The 941 existing rows did not change; two rows were added.

| Case | Punish.Kill points |
|---|---:|
| Plain kill | 5 |
| Headshot, sniper, precision artillery | 5 |
| Explosive kill | 4 |
| Claymore kill | 1 (0.5 raised to `PointsMin` 1) |
| Environment kill | 1 (0.5 raised to `PointsMin` 1) |
| Teamkill flagged | the cause value divided by 2.5: plain and headshot 2, Claymore 0.4 |
| Forgive | 500 cash |

The formula `clamp((5 + 0) * CauseMultiplier, 1, 5) / HalvingDivisor` clamps before it divides, so Claymore and environment kills are 1 point, not 0.5 as round 42 stated; a flagged teamkill divides the clamped value by 2.5.

## Wiki updates

- `team-offence-challenges.md`: Punish gives 5 points for a plain, headshot, sniper or artillery kill, 4 for an explosive kill and 1 for a Claymore or environment kill (the earlier 0.5 was the value before the minimum of 1); a flagged teamkill divides that by 2.5 (2, 2, 1.6 for explosive, 0.4 for Claymore and environment). Forgive pays 500 cash.
- Economy, refunds: a refund pays 90% (rounded) of the smaller of what you spent on the refunded items and the eligible purchase amount, within a window of 20 score ticks (config values 0.9 and 20).
- Shop prices: the final price is the base price times every applicable discount ratio multiplied together; a ratio of zero makes the item free; the shop shows the combined change. Do not say how the ini values 50 / 80 / 90 become ratios as fact until the P1 slot is read.
- Vendor: 343 of 670 vendor variations are hidden tiles (ammunition and magazine variants and a few items); `LevelToUnlock` is 0 or 1 everywhere, so it gates nothing; shop gating is by Role / Wardog level and required items.
- Passive missions: the Time Played mission records the real time elapsed every 5 seconds.
- Weight: carried weight is item weight times 1000 (integer); the weight classes start at carried totals of 10, 17, 27 and 40 item-weight units (Light, Medium, Heavy, Super Heavy).
- Destruction: global force thresholds 1200 (building chunk) and 400 (decorator) and a 400 vehicle-hit explosion radius; the explosive decorator whitelist is empty by configuration; chunk prefabs hold no health; one apartment corridor-end chunk is indestructible.
- Do not publish the FOB health (300 or 15,000) until B21 is read.
