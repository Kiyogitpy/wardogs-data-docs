The `Z_Construct` getter (round 44) is only passed by address (`lea reg,[getter]`), never called, so a call scan finds no caller. ## Wiki updates

- Buildables, FOB: health is 15,000 (the Blueprint's 300 is overwritten at spawn by the data asset's `MaxHealth`, applied on the server to both base and maximum health). Other buildables use their data-asset health as already listed.
- Building rewards: the FOB deposit message (build supplies placed into the FOB) is sent by the FOB's item-added handler (server); the build/repair action sends the "building" and "building finished" messages; the buildable's `OnKilled` sends the "destroyed" and "destroyed assist" messages. The "build supplies consumed" and "schematic placed in FOB" messages have a reward row and state tree but no sender was found in the game code read; do not say when they fire.
- Skills: a skill's number comes from its curve evaluated at the skill node's upgrade level (level 0 = no effect, value 1.0; keys at 2, 3, 4 give the larger steps; levels between keys are blended). Say "skill level", not "points" or "tier".
- Destruction: the chunk and decorator thresholds are 1,200 and 400 "thousand" of an internal speed-times-scale value; damage events of types flagged to destroy ignore them (treated as infinite force). Do not state a unit (newtons, kg cm/s).
- Do not publish: what loads the two building mesh-to-prefab lookups (no reader found); the physical unit of the collision force.
