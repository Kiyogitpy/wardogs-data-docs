## Wiki updates

- `_wiki/game-mechanics/` sight / ADS page (wherever the ADS zoom is described): add the camera rule. At full ADS the view angle is `F / M` degrees in the default "Affected" aiming-FOV setting (F = the player's field of view, M = the sight's magnification), or `72 / M` degrees in the "Independent" setting (then the FOV slider no longer changes the scope view). Iron sights count as M = 1.2 plus the focus bonus. The view narrows along the weapon's ADS blend curve and eases at the weapon's FOV interpolation speed (20 per second when unset). Old: "how the zoom becomes the camera FOV is not known" -> closed.
- `_wiki/game-mechanics/vehicles.md` or `movement.md` (leaving a moving vehicle): the exit damage depends on the vehicle speed in **km/h** at the moment you leave, applied when you land: 0 at 40 km/h and below, 40 at 50, 85 at 60, 100 at 75 and above (linear between). Below the vehicle's minimum speed for damage nothing is applied (its value is not in the data exports). Old: "speed unit not known" -> km/h.
- `_wiki/game-mechanics/` action-system or character-actions page (if one lists response types): Interrupt cancels the listed active actions; InterruptAndBuffer cancels them and re-queues them; InterruptBuffered clears queued copies; InterruptCategories cancels every active action that shares a listed category; Reactivate stops the listed active actions and restarts them (with their saved context) once the new action is in place; InterruptOnComplete cancels the listed actions when the new action ends. Remove "cancel rules not known".
- `_wiki/guides/aiming-the-sph-2.md` and `_wiki/game-mechanics/artillery.md`: the mil pointer reads the turret's own elevation (relative to the hull), not the horizon; on flat ground they are the same. The range function's 16.8 mrad term is unchanged. Remove "zero of the pointer not located".
