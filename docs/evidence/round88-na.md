## Method

- Corpus: `corpus.sqlite` tables `types`/`members` for the SDK offsets (`UBHActionDefinition+188` obfuscated name `_aaaapkblfhgmbaoodpfni`), `functions` and `code/` for decompiled bodies. Helper scripts live in `E:/r88/work/na/` (`q.py` body lookup, `scan.py`/`scan2..6.py` capstone scans over the restored exe, `refs.py` rip-relative and call cross references, `disa.py` disassembly with corpus names, `strs.py` string literals near a function).
- Item 1/2: `reg_*` UFunction thunks (`0x5267D20`-`0x5269C30`) lead to the implementation in `0x5250000`-`0x5268000`. The reader of byte `+0xBC` was found by a scan of `0x5240000`-`0x5270000` for `[reg+0xBC]` (three hits; only `0x524ECB0` reads it; the other two are stores). The stub table at `0x5251190`-`0x52511E0` maps each prediction-buffer lambda to its body.
- Item 4: scan of `0x8690000`-`0x86B0000` for `[reg+0x2D0]`; `0x86A40E0` is the only one in the class's own code with a float read, and its callee `0x86A3D70` is the helper.
- Item 5: tag identities from the registration functions at `0x1290419` and `0x12900F9` (the preceding string literal in each); `bUseOutOfBounds` and the rectangle from `members` (`AWDWorldSettings+1724`) and the disassembly of `0x6C051F0` (`movzx eax, [rcx+0x6BC]`) and `0x5360B60` (`lea rax, [rcx+0x6C0]`).
- Item 6: slot comparison between the base `UWDPlayerSpawnManagerComponent` vtable and the Deathmatch one in the `aliases` table (differences at slots 0, 128, 159, 160).
