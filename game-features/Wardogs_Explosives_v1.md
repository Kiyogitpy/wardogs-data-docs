# Wardogs: Explosives Reference (what every explosive does, in numbers)

One page for every explosion in the export: hand grenades, the 40 mm grenade, launcher rockets, vehicle cannon and rocket shells, artillery, the drone, the explosive arrow, C4, IED, AT mine, Claymore and the Molotov burst. The tables are generated by [`_tools/explosive_tables.py`](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/blob/main/_tools/explosive_tables.py) (`--write`) from numbers that other pages already document; this page only joins them. The item side (price, weight, throw velocity) is on [Item Shop](Wardogs_ItemShop_v1.md) section 4 and [Weapon Shop](Wardogs_WeaponShop_v1.md) (launchers).

Which props these explosions can demolish (damage-source tags and minimum distances per ruleset) is on [Demolition Assets, rulesets](../content/Wardogs_DemolitionAssets_v1.md).

## Sources and labels

| Label | Source |
|---|---|
| **Data** | Projectile assets (`WDProjectileData` under `Content/GameMechanics/Inventory/Equipment/Caliber`, plus the drone, arrow, 70 mm and TV-guided assets): `BaseDamage`, `ExplosiveRadius`, `EffectiveExplosiveRadius`, `ExplosiveDamageFallOff`, `MinimumExplosiveDamage`, `bPreferUpwardsTrace`, `ExplosiveBluntDamage`, `bHasArmingDelay` / `ArmingDelay`. Thrown and placed actors: the reviewed defaults in [`assets/data/explosive-actors.json`](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/blob/main/assets/data/explosive-actors.json) ([evidence](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/blob/main/docs/evidence/round8-explosive-actors.md)). Rule rows: `DT_GlobalDamage_InfantryData`, `DT_GlobalDamage_VehicleData*`. |
| **Native** | The radial amount rule, the Inner/Outer ring tags, the line-of-sight and trace behavior, and thrown-contact damage: [Damage Pipeline](../pipelines/Wardogs_DamagePipeline_v2.md) section 1.1 (radial falloff, radial distance and gather, projectile blast context). Not repeated here. |
| **Derived** | Every damage-to-target column: the amounts above run through the existing code paths (`_tools/infantry_tables.py` rule evaluation for characters, `_tools/damage_tables.py` rule factor and section routing for vehicles). |

Units: radii are exported in centimetres and shown in metres. Damage values are starting amounts before the rule rows. A source row is a projectile asset or an actor, not an item: one asset can be fired by several items, and "Fired or placed by" lists the items found by `_tools/infantry_tables.py` (the weapon and vehicle-weapon items whose caliber is that projectile id). Assets with no item are listed anyway and marked.

Not in these tables: the two vehicle cannons `20x102mm` and `35x228mm` (they carry `EffectiveExplosiveRadius` but no `ExplosiveRadius`, and an absent radius means no blast; see [Infantry Viewer](../pipelines/infantry-viewer.md)), launcher back-blast and the vehicle explosion that hurts occupants (both in [Damage Pipeline](../pipelines/Wardogs_DamagePipeline_v2.md) section 1.1).

## 1. Blast numbers

**Data**, `explosive_tables main`. Base is the amount from the centre out to the inner radius E. Beyond E the amount falls linearly toward Base x (1 - fall-off) at the outer radius R, floors at Minimum (a "-" is no floor) and is 0 at or beyond R (**Native**, Damage Pipeline section 1.1, "Radial falloff"). "Trace" is `bPreferUpwardsTrace`, the setting the gather reads; the line-of-sight behavior it feeds is described in the same section ("Radial distance and gather"). Thrown and placed actors pass line of sight true (**Native**, [explosive-actor evidence](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/blob/main/docs/evidence/round8-explosive-actors.md)).

**Anomaly (Data).** The `122mm.Havoc` asset has `ExplosiveRadius` 300 and `EffectiveExplosiveRadius` 600, so E is larger than R. The native rule takes the Inner branch first, so the amount is the full 300 out to 3 m and 0 beyond; the pair looks swapped in the export. No weapon item in the export lists this asset, and only the asset itself and two UI tables reference its tag, so it has no gameplay effect (Damage Pipeline O-9, closed as unused data).

## 2. Fuse, arming and direct impact

**Data**, `explosive_tables direct`. Projectiles arm after `ArmingDelay` seconds of flight when `bHasArmingDelay` is true; before that a direct hit does `ExplosiveBluntDamage` instead (Damage Pipeline section 7.6 and the `--explosives` table of `_tools/infantry_tables.py`). Frag, C4, IED and Molotov also inherit thrown-contact `ImpactDamage` 10 (**Native**, path `0x6CA8730`). The grenade base class sets a default fuse of 5.0 s (**Native**, `0x6CADC90`); the per-item overrides read from the Blueprints are in the fuse table below. Mine triggers, C4 detonation range and the IED delay are on [Fire, Smoke and Mines](Wardogs_FireAndUtility_v1.md) ([evidence](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/blob/main/docs/evidence/round8-explosive-actors.md)).

## 4. Damage to an unarmored infantry torso

**Derived**, `explosive_tables infantry`. The amount from section 3 times the rule factor for the ring the hit falls in, evaluated for `Torso.Upper` with the same code the [Infantry Viewer](../pipelines/infantry-viewer.md) uses (`encode_zones` and `decode` in `_tools/infantry_tables.py`; blast hits never get the `ExplosiveBlunt` bone entries, so most rings are a flat factor). The context is the source tag plus the context the infantry viewer retains for that source (weapon tag where an item exists), `DamageType.Explosive` and the Inner or Outer ring tag. "Mid-radius" is half of R; the ring there is Inner when R/2 is within E. Assumptions are the damage-table ones: hostile target, no armor, no instigator tags, no ricochet, line of sight clear, one component hit per body part. A real blast can hit several components of one character, and each kept hit is its own evaluation (Damage Pipeline section 1.1, "On a character"); this table is one hit.

## 5. Vehicle point-blank hits to destroy

**Derived**, `explosive_tables vehicles`. Hits to destroy the vehicle's main health section with the explosion centred on it (distance 0, so the Inner ring and the full Base amount), with the rule factor in brackets. The factor uses the vehicle's own damage row plus the generic row for the source tag, its context and `Radius.Inner` (`source_rule_factor` in `_tools/damage_tables.py`), applied once to the main-body section; section routing then runs with each section's radial skip flags honored (`hits_to_destroy`, [Vehicle Damage Pipeline](../pipelines/Wardogs_VehicleDamagePipeline_v1.md) section 5.4). A "-" means the rules reduce the damage to 0 or it never reaches the main health.

Assumption, stated plainly: one radial application resolving to the main health section. The [Vehicle Viewer](../pipelines/vehicle-viewer.md) resolves the section from the first component hit and lets each component hit evaluate separately, so a real blast near several parts can do more than one hit's worth; this table is the single-hit case. Blast falloff, line of sight and overlap multiplicity at larger distances are not modelled (the vehicle catalog note says the same, Vehicle Damage Pipeline section 5.3). Vehicles: Little Bird `ROT_03_Default`, Humvee `WHL_05_Humvee_Default`, T-72 `TNK_04`, Leopard spawn `SPW_01`.

## Open items

- Per-item fuse overrides and the mine and Claymore trigger defaults are settled in round 17 (fuse table above, [Fire, Smoke and Mines](Wardogs_FireAndUtility_v1.md) section 5). Still open: whether a thrown Molotov bursts on impact or on its fuse, and what the frag `GrenadeInteractionData.MinimumRemainingFuseTime` 5.0 (above the 4.0 s fuse) gates.
- Which vehicle weapons fire `70mm`, `84mm.TVGuided`, `122mm.Havoc` and the two sandbox shells is not mapped.
- Armored targets and several-component blasts are not tabulated; use the [Infantry Viewer](../pipelines/infantry-viewer.md) and [Vehicle Viewer](../pipelines/vehicle-viewer.md).
