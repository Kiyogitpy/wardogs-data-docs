# Fixed-wing flight model (FWG_01)

*Source: the five `DA_FWG_01_*` flight assets, `Curve_Symmetrical_LiftCoefFromAngleOfAttackDeg`, `Curve_DragCoefFromAngleOfAttackDeg`, `Curve_FWG_01_NoseCenterWheel_SteeringInfluenceByForwardSpeedKmPH` and `CurveTable_FWG_01_AutoTrimByAirspeedKmPH` under `Content/Vehicles/Pawns/Air/Airplane/FWG_01/Data`. Generated by [`_tools/vehicle_round85_tables.py`](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/blob/main/_tools/vehicle_round85_tables.py). Evidence and status: [round 85 vehicles](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/blob/main/docs/evidence/round85-veh.md).*

FWG_01 is the unfinished two-seat airplane described in [Fixed-wing airplane](../_wiki/vehicles/fixed-wing-airplane.md). [Vehicles (reference)](../content/Wardogs_Vehicles_v2.md) section 3 and 4 list its mass, thrust, airfoil areas and control mapping. This page adds the rest: the property names, the full drag and torque set, the curve keys, the auto-trim table and the control-assist stacks. The data stores **no top speed and no stall speed**; the speed envelope in section 6 is derived and labelled so.

## 6. Auto-trim table and derived speed envelope

### 6a. Auto-trim table (`CurveTable_FWG_01_AutoTrimByAirspeedKmPH`)

The pitch assist samples one of 22 curves; the row names are `Gear_{0,1}_Thrust_{0..100 step 10}`, so the curve is picked by landing-gear state and throttle in tens of percent (the pick rule is native and not decoded). Each curve maps airspeed in km/h to a pitch-control trim value (stored as shown, unit not stated). Every curve rises to +0.2 within 10 km/h, holds it up to a throttle-dependent speed, then falls through 0 and ends at -0.08 or -0.09 at 1200 km/h. The throttle-0 rows are flat at -0.09. ### 6b. Derived speed envelope (physics model)

**Derived, not stored.** The data has no speed limit, so this estimates it from the engine thrust, the lift and drag curves and the airfoil areas. Inputs: mass 10,000 (`MassOverride`, read as kg), thrust 226,800 N (`MaxEngineThrust_n`), `MainLiftMultiplier` 0.4, air density 1.25 kg/m3 (`AirDensityAtSeaLevel` 0.00125 read as g/cm3), g = 9.81 m/s2 (assumed), the twelve airfoils without the airbrake (its force is 0 while retracted), lift area 520 m2 (all airfoils whose lift direction is up; the two rudders lift sideways) and drag area 600 m2 (all eleven).

Formulas, with q = 0.5 x rho x v^2 and alpha the airplane pitch angle:

- Lift = q x MainLiftMultiplier x sum over lifting airfoils of (area x LiftCurve(alpha + incidence)).
- Drag = q x sum over all eleven airfoils of (area x DragCurve(alpha + incidence)). The body drag fields of section 2 are left out because the way they combine is not decoded (read as cm2 they add under 1% at these speeds).
- Level flight: pick alpha so that lift = mass x g. Required thrust = drag at that alpha. Stall speed uses the alpha where the area-weighted lift is greatest.
- No propeller model: thrust is taken as a constant 0 to 226,800 N, independent of speed. Control surfaces are neutral and the tail lifts at the same angle as the wing.


**Stall (derived).** The lift sum peaks at alpha = 14.9 degrees with an area-weighted coefficient of 207.3 m2, giving a level stall speed of **99 km/h** (27.5 m/s) with the airbrake in.

Reading: the physics model puts the drag equal to full thrust between 850 and 900 km/h (about 880 km/h) and stalls at about 99 km/h. The auto-trim table instead centres full throttle on 430 km/h. The two disagree by a factor of two or more, so at least one input (the unit of the thrust, the area, or the density) is read wrongly, or the game relies on an effect not in the data. Treat 430 km/h at full throttle as the number the developers tuned for and the physics figures as an unvalidated upper bound. Neither is a stored top speed.
