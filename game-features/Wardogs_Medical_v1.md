# Wardogs: Medical and Healing (what each item does, in numbers)

*Source: the item definitions of every medical item under [`data/Wardogs`](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/tree/main/data/Wardogs), the GameplayEffect values in [`assets/data/ge-values.json`](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/blob/main/assets/data/ge-values.json), the Medic skill rows and curves, and a few native constants read from the decompile corpus (CL-516586, image base `0x140000000`, addresses are RVAs). Every table below is generated by [`_tools/medical_tables.py`](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/blob/main/_tools/medical_tables.py); do not edit it by hand.*

The [Item Shop](Wardogs_ItemShop_v1.md) lists what medical items cost and the [Item Actions](../content/Wardogs_ItemActions_v1.md) page lists the use actions. This page joins them with the gameplay effects and the downed and revive rules to answer: how long does each use take, what is consumed, how much does it heal, and what else does it do. The Medic skills are also on [Progression Tables](Wardogs_Progression_v1.md).

## Sources and labels

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|

| **Native** | read from the decompiled game code; these are class defaults, and the cooked blueprint may override them (the player blueprint is not in `data/`) |

## 1. What the data says, in short

- **Healing is per use, not a rate, except for the Medical Bag.** The bandage adds 35 health and the Individual First Aid Kit adds 100 per use (**Data**). The Medical Bag is the only item with a stated duration: 35 over 5 s on yourself, 15 over 1.5 s on a teammate (**Data**).
- **There is no damage-over-time bleeding.** "Bleed" in the game is the downed bleed-out timer (section 6). No medical item definition has a cure or removal tag, and no effect below changes health over time (**Data**). No splint or tourniquet item exists in the data.
- **Using an item slows you.** The bandage, First Aid Kit, Medical Bag, stim and resuscitator apply `GE_UseAppliable`, which multiplies max speed by 0.5 (**Data**).
- **Use time in seconds is the length of the animation montage.** Most items use `AnimationLength`; the base action reads the length of the item's animation (`CalculateTotalInteractionTime` in `B_Action_Appliable_Base`, **Data (Blueprint bytecode)**), or the position of a named notify for `AnimationNotifier`. The montage lengths are in the use table below (**Data (Blueprint CDO)**, round 17 export, [evidence](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/blob/main/docs/evidence/round17-blueprint-defaults.md)): bandage 5.23 s on yourself and 4.83 s on a teammate, First Aid Kit 5.23 s with the heal at 4.73 s, Enox 2.17 s, Stim Pen 2 s. Every montage has play rate 1.0. The Medical Bag and the resuscitator run until the item is empty or the target is full.
- **Natural regeneration for infantry was not found.** The vitality component only starts a 10 s regen delay timer after damage (**Native**); the one regen component in the data belongs to Deathmatch and its values are blueprint (not traced).

Notes:

- **Bandage and kit amounts** are the item's `AttributeValue` with `bApplyAsDelta` true (added to current health). Whether the amount lands at the animation notify or spreads over the animation is blueprint (`B_Action_Appliable_Base`) and not traced. The First Aid Kit text says it heals back to full; the data value is +100, which reaches full from any health at 100 max health (Derived).
- **Stim Pen** does not heal. Its `AttributeValue` is unset; it applies `GE_UnlimitedTacSprint` (section 5). Shop text: "Unlimited Tactical Sprint for Short Time; Reduces Equipment Weight Class" (**Data**). The tutorial text says it temporarily increases sprint and tac sprint speed.
- **Defibrillator** is blocked while the user is `Vitality.Critical` (action response rules, **Data**). The shop text says it revives instantly and needs a battery.
- **Resuscitators** are single use per purchase, with a faster revive than bare hands (shop and tutorial text, **Data**).

The rate columns are Derived: amount divided by duration. ## 6. Downed, bleed-out, revive and regeneration

What the tutorial says (**Data**, `DA_Tutorials_Health`): "When your health reaches zero, you enter the critical state. If you aren't revived in time, you'll bleed out and respawn at Headquarters." and "Reviving with no medical gear takes time, so watch your surroundings." A critical player can be dragged by a friend. `IA_Player_DownedHoldOn`, `IA_Player_DownedGiveUp` and `IA_Player_DownedCallForHelp` are the downed inputs (**Data**).

Reading of the bleed-out values (**Derived** from the player Blueprint defaults above): the timer starts at 360 s and drains at 0.5 per second in the normal critical state, so a downed player lasts 720 s unless someone helps; a player who was revived once and goes down again restarts at no less than 30 s, which drains in 60 s. Giving up switches the rate to 240 per second, so a full timer ends in 1.5 s. The game defaults (300 s, 60 s, 0.1, 6.0) are overridden by the Blueprint. What the `IncreaseBleedTime` skill changes is not traced.
