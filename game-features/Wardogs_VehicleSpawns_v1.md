# Wardogs: Vehicle Spawns (deployment boards, vehicle yards and infantry spawn areas per map)

*Source: the 61 world and World Partition cell packages that name the spawn volume class, plus the 5 further cells that hold a deployment board or spawn manager without a volume, the board Blueprint defaults, the experience configs under [`data/Wardogs/Config/Experiences`](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/tree/main/data/Wardogs/Config/Experiences) and the VehicleUtility ini. Everything below is generated by [`_tools/vehicle_spawns.py`](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/blob/main/_tools/vehicle_spawns.py) into [`assets/data/vehicle-spawns.json`](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/blob/main/assets/data/vehicle-spawns.json); do not edit this page by hand. Evidence level: cooked data read, nothing run.*

Round 42 found 110 spawn volumes in the world data ([evidence](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/blob/main/docs/evidence/round42-modes-ai-other.md)). This page ties them to maps: each volume sits in a World Partition cell that belongs to a **spawn data layer** (for example `DL_GranadaSpawn`), and each King of the Hill entry in the experience configs enables one base layer plus **three spawn layers**, one per team slot ([Experiences](Wardogs_GameFeatures_Experiences_v2.md)). So a match uses three spawn sites per map, and each site is one layer.

## 1. What a spawn layer holds

Every spawn layer holds the same kit for one team slot (Alpha, Bravo or Charlie in the data):

- **One deployment board** (`B_VehicleDeploymentBoard_<faction>_C`). It carries 4 to 6 vehicle spots (`VehicleSpawnLocations`, target points) and spawns the faction's APC (the [M113 APC SV](../_wiki/vehicles/m113-apc-sv.md) in the Lonestar, Valkyra or Manticore version, set by `SpawnVehicleTag`). Players deploy onto that vehicle; the board refuses a deployment when the player is driving, has low health or the vehicle has no free seat (`Interactive.Deployment.Rejection.Driving`, `.LowHP`, `.NoSeat`).
- **Three to six spawn volumes** (`WDSpawnVolume`): normally an APC volume (the spots of the board), one to three ground-vehicle yards, one air yard and one light-vehicle (Bobcat) yard. A few layers lack one of these (section 3). Each lists the vehicle tags it allows and excludes. The `TargetPoints` of a volume are the vehicle spots; `MaxSpawnPoints` and the spacing fields are separate generation settings whose use was not traced (0 on many volumes).
- **One infantry spawn manager** (`B_SpawnManager_C`) with a `FactionTag` and a grid of 46 to 81 spawn transforms (200 cm spacing, the nine-by-nine grid trimmed to the terrain). This is the infantry spawn area of that team slot. The spawn query asset (`EQS_SpawnQuery_800_Bravo`, an 800 cm search) is set on 13 of the 21 managers.

Totals per world are in section 6. The Kenya entry enables only its base layer (`DL_Kenya`) and has no world package under `Maps/Worlds` in the client (no Kenya or Africa map files at all); Sniper Showdown and the Tevza entry enable no spawn layer that holds a volume (Tevza names three spawn layers that no dumped cell contains).

## 7. Limits, timers and data quirks

- **Per-player vehicle limit.** `WDVehicleUtilityComponent.MaxVehiclesPerPlayer = 2` ([`DefaultVehicleUtility.ini`](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/blob/main/data/Wardogs/Plugins/GameFeatures/Vehicles/VehicleUtility/Config/DefaultVehicleUtility.ini)); the plugin text says it caps vehicle ownership per player on the session game mode.
- **Deploy cooldown.** `PlayerDeployCooldown` is 600.0 s in the board Blueprint default and no map board overrides it. `RadarRestoreHealthThreshold` is 0.55.
- **Vehicle respawn timer: not in the data.** The board class has `VehicleRespawnTimer`, `LosingVehicleRespawnTimer`, `VehicleHealthThreshold` and `VehicleSpeedThreshold` ([SDK](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/blob/main/sdk/raw/WDGame_classes.hpp) `0x3F8..0x40C`), but neither the Blueprint default nor any of the 18 map boards sets them, so the values are native constructor defaults (not read). The Gym test boards set 240 s respawn, 120 s deploy cooldown, health threshold 0 and speed threshold 1.
- **No per-yard vehicle count or per-vehicle respawn timer.** A volume lists allowed tags and spots; how many of each vehicle exist and how long a destroyed vehicle takes to return is not in the cooked data (the spawn logic is native; see the round 42 note on the open volume route).
- **Boards and APC tags disagree in places.** Board classes against the team slot of their layer: Lonestar 5 of 6 on Alpha, Manticore 7 of 8 on Charlie, Valkyra 6 of 6 on Bravo (one Valkyra board has no APC volume to compare). The exceptions are in Russia, where the Kuzminki layer (Alpha) has a Manticore board and the Ramenki layer (Charlie) a Lonestar board. Volumes carry a different APC tag from their team slot in three places (all in Europe, all the Valkyra tag: `DL_StuttgartSpawn`, `DL_CalaisSpawn`, `DL_HanoverSpawn`). Which side of the pair wins at run time is not known.
- **One Detroit air yard belongs to no spawn layer.** An Alpha air yard with four explicit helicopter families (Havoc, Little Bird family, Huey, Z20 Lakota family) sits in a cell with no data layer, so it is part of the base world for every Detroit entry (inferred from the empty layer list of its cell).
- **Strike and Disarm.** One infantry volume (Charlie, 15 max points) and one spawn manager sit in the Strike and Disarm external layer on Kavkazi; they are not part of the King of the Hill spawns. Further spawn managers sit in the PvE scenario layers (ScenarioExample, VIP Escort, Strike and Disarm); they were not dumped.

## 8. Reproduce

```
WdExtract <Paks> <key> <usmap> <out> <Paks>\oo2core_9_win64.dll findname Wardogs WDSpawnVolume      -> findname.txt (61 packages)
WdExtract ... <out>\sv dump <those package paths>
WdExtract ... findname Wardogs B_SpawnManager_C   (and B_VehicleDeploymentBoard_Lonestar_C / _Manticore_C / _Valkrya_C)
WdExtract ... <out>\sv_extra dump <the cells not already in sv>
WdExtract ... list B_VehicleDeploymentBoard, then dump it plus .../Gamemode/Objects/B_SpawnManager into <out>\bd
python -I _tools/vehicle_spawns.py [--check] <round42 dir with sv> <dir bd>   (sv_extra is read next to bd)
```

Extractor arguments are in [`tools/cue4parse_extractor/README.md`](../tools/cue4parse_extractor/README.md). The wiki articles for each map summarise this page.
