# Vehicle turret controls

*Source: every `WDVehicleWeaponDataAsset` and every `DT_VehicleCrosshair_*` table under [`Plugins/GameFeatures/Vehicles`](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/tree/main/data/Wardogs/Plugins/GameFeatures/Vehicles). Generated by [`_tools/vehicle_round85_tables.py`](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/blob/main/_tools/vehicle_round85_tables.py). Evidence and status: [round 85 vehicles](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/blob/main/docs/evidence/round85-veh.md).*

Each vehicle weapon is a `WDVehicleWeaponDataAsset` (`W_*`) that holds the weapon tag, the turret mesh, the muzzle sockets, the aim-rotation settings and the camera for each aim state. The damage and ballistics are on the `WE_*` weapon-extension assets, which are covered in [Vehicle weapons and effects](Wardogs_VehicleWeaponsEffects_v1.md). `-` means the asset does not set the field.


## 1. Rotation sensitivity and limits
