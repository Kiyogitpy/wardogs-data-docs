# Wardogs: Weapon Shop, Attachments and Components

Structured tables for the weapon bundles under [`Weapons/Attachments`, `Weapons/Components` and `Weapons/Platforms`](https://github.com/Kiyogitpy/wardogs-data-docs/tree/main/data/Wardogs/Plugins/GameFeatures/Weapons). The tables are generated by `_tools/weapon_vendor_tables.py` (`--write`). They sit next to the folder summary in [Weapons (feature)](Wardogs_GameFeatures_Weapons_v2.md) section 6, and the final attribute values in [Weapon Attributes](../content/Wardogs_WeaponAttributes_v1.md). Armor, helmets, backpacks, medical items, throwables, explosives, tools and vehicles are in [Item Shop](Wardogs_ItemShop_v1.md); ammo is in [Items](../content/Wardogs_Items_v2.md).

## Sources and labels

| Label | Source |
|---|---|
| **Data** | `ID_*` item definitions (`BHInventoryItemDefinition`): item id, attachment type tag, sub-slot containers, default items. |
| **Data** | `DT_EntityData_*` (`BHEntityData_UI`): display name, subtitle, shop text. `DT_VendorData_*` and `DT_WeaponVendor*` (`WDVendorData`): price, unlock level, required items, buy limit, variations. |
| **Derived** | Fit groups and caliber. A weapon accepts an item when the item passes the sub-slot filter of one of the weapon's containers, or of a container of an attachment that fits (3 levels, same rule as [Weapon Attributes](../content/Wardogs_WeaponAttributes_v1.md)). Caliber is the allow-list of the weapon's `Chamber` container. |
| **Native** | none used. The slot filter itself is documented in the Weapon Attributes page (RVA `0x67A2B40`). |

The vendor rows hold no currency field. "Price" is the `Price` of the first variation with `bShowInVendor = true`, in the unit the vendor UI uses (not decoded here). "Variations" is how many variations the row has; the others are hidden price points and are not decoded. "Req. items" is `UnlockRequiredItems` of the shown variation.

## 1. Weapon platforms

Launcher rows (9K333 Verba, MAAWS, MGL-40, RPG-7): the rocket and grenade damage, radii, falloff and damage to infantry and vehicles are in [Explosives](Wardogs_Explosives_v1.md).

One row per weapon definition with a stats asset. Price, level and category come from the weapon's vendor row. Default attachments are the items the weapon's containers start with (`DefaultContainerItems`, else `DefaultItems`), including sub-slots of those items.
